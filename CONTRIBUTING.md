# Contributing to Project Intersection Public Review

The highest-value contributions are independent checks, stronger prior art, counterexamples, reproduction failures, and evidence-boundary corrections.
**Source, copyright, contributor and future-continuity respect:** [Original 1 English / 한국어 원본2](TECHNOLOGY_PROGRESS_CONTRIBUTOR_CONTINUITY_EN_KO.md). New or promoted research should identify upstream work, original author vs earliest originator, actual rights holder/license scope where verified, the contributor's actual delta, and evidence-bounded benefit/cost/compensation/exit/succession outcomes. Automated maintainers should reuse existing Git/source records; do not demand repeated personal proof, health details or uncompensated correction from contributors. / 출처·권리·기여·보상·이탈·후세의 기록은 기존 증거를 재사용하며 개인의 추가 무보수 노동을 강제하지 않는다.


## Preferred contribution types

1. independent rerun of a released executable package;
2. independent implementation from specification without project-code reuse;
3. contradiction or reproducibility report;
4. stronger prior art that narrows Project novelty;
5. historical/empirical evidence with explicit identification limits;
6. negative result that changes a claim status.

## Minimum provenance

Please include:

- exact Project artifact / commit tested;
- environment and commands;
- whether Project code was reused;
- observed result;
- mismatch, if any;
- source links or DOI where relevant;
- conflicts of interest or shared lineage that could affect independence.

Do not label a same-model summary, Project-authored rerun, or CI pass as independent validation.

## Knowledge-graph changes

A structured entity must link to a source artifact. The graph is an index, not a superior truth source.

For external material, add citation metadata and a rights/license note. Do not copy article bodies, figures, tables, website CSS, icons, or proprietary datasets merely because they are publicly accessible.

## Privacy

Do not submit unnecessary health, family, banking, tax, credential, or private conversation information.


## Public research scope / 공개 연구 범위

Only publish material with a traceable connection to Project Intersection's questions, methods, prior art, falsifiable cases, evidence, governance, or research funding. Do not import unrelated personal conversations as evidence. Exclude personal financial positions, securities transactions, price forecasts, account/debt details, and unrelated life or consumer topics. Removing such material does **not** justify suppressing relevant counterexamples, negative results, contributor costs, or scientific uncertainties.

Economic incentives, institutional finance and investment as *research mechanisms* remain in scope when they have a documented causal relevance and proper source attribution; they are not personal trading advice. During cleanup, preserve in-scope provenance, stable identifiers and Git rollback capability. Do not repeat private details in pull-request descriptions or audit logs.

공개 자료에는 Project Intersection의 연구 질문·방법·선행연구·반증 가능한 사례·증거·거버넌스·연구지원과 연결되는 내용만 수록한다. 사적인 대화를 연구 근거로 전용하지 않으며 개인 금융 포지션·증권 거래·가격 예측·계좌·부채 및 연구와 무관한 생활 주제는 제외한다. 그러나 **연구상 관련된** 경제적 유인, 제도적 금융·투자 기제, 기여 비용, 반례, 부정 결과와 불확실성은 출처와 인과적 관련성을 확인하여 보존한다. 정리 과정에서 식별자·기여 계보·되돌리기 가능성을 유지하고 공개 변경 기록에 사적 세부사항을 재기재하지 않는다.

## Result interpretation

A successful reproduction strengthens only the narrow reproduced claim.

A failed reproduction, stronger counterexample, or prior-art correction is a valid and useful contribution.
