## Suggested independent reviewer protocol
1. Read [public overview](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/blob/main/RESEARCH_OVERVIEW.md), [evidence index](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/blob/main/PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md), [status policy](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/blob/main/RESEARCH_STATUS_POLICY.md), and [attribution policy](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/blob/main/ATTRIBUTION_AND_CONTRIBUTION_BOUNDARY.md). Read [prior art](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/blob/main/PRIOR_ART_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) before claiming novelty.
2. Select a **narrow** public claim; locate the original evidence and its provenance in the [review packet](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/blob/main/PUBLIC_REVIEW_PACKET.md).
3. Preserve the actual evidence status. E007 is project-lineage synthetic computation, E008 has no reported result, and E009 preserves a synthetic negative result. Do not upgrade these to external replication.
4. Report: (a) exact claim, (b) strongest supporting evidence, (c) strongest counterexample/prior art, (d) uncertainty, (e) what is genuinely new if anything, (f) whether the assessment is independent of project-generated material.
5. Distinguish **founder/researcher contribution**, **AI-assisted production**, **external prior art**, **scientific support**, **independent validation**, and **financial claims**. No one score cancels these distinctions.
6. Do not ask the original contributor to perform additional free validation. Independent validation requires an actually independent evaluator.
