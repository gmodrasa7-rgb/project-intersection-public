## Original 1 — English Canonical

### Objective

Convert the current public research layer into a compact due-diligence and reproduction package that an outside reviewer can test without relying on private conversation history.

### Budget

| Use | USD |
|---|---:|
| Research / engineering time | 3,200 |
| API / compute | 1,000 |
| Independent attack / review | 500 |
| Documentation / hosting / packaging | 300 |
| **Total** | **5,000** |

### 30-day deliverables

1. **Evidence map**
   - maintain [PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md](../PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md);
   - distinguish synthetic, executable, reproduced, externally rerun, independently implemented, and empirically validated states.

2. **Reproduction package**
   - harden the released E007 package;
   - preserve exact commands, environment assumptions, expected outputs, known failure lineage, and falsification conditions.

3. **Adversarial test layer**
   - add reviewer-safe tests targeting timing, exit semantics, evaluator capture, substitution, and role-reversal assumptions;
   - publish negative results where the proposed mechanism fails.

4. **Claim ledger**
   - expose a compact KEEP / MODIFY / KILL / HOLD trail for the public claims touched during the sprint;
   - separate novelty status from scientific-support status.

5. **External challenge**
   - allocate a bounded budget for a separate reviewer or attacker where available;
   - do not label project-internal reruns as independent scientific replication.

6. **End-of-sprint decision memo**
   - list what strengthened, what weakened, what was killed, what remains unresolved, and what evidence would justify a 90-day follow-on.

### 30-day gate

A follow-on should **not** be justified merely because more material was produced.

The bridge is successful only if at least one of the following occurs:

- verification cost falls materially;
- a claim is narrowed or killed by a stronger test;
- an outside reviewer can reproduce or attack a public artifact with less dependence on the founder;
- evidence lineage becomes more auditable;
- a previously ambiguous failure boundary becomes measurable.

### Anti-overclaim rule

The sprint will not present:

- internal model agreement as independent evidence;
- a passing test as proof of a broad real-world theory;
- planned review as completed review;
- a funding draft as awarded money.

### 30일 산출물

1. **증거 지도**
   - [PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md](../PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) 유지;
   - 합성, 실행가능, 재현, 외부 재실행, 독립 구현, 현실검증 상태를 분리.

2. **재현 패키지**
   - 공개 E007 묶음 강화;
   - 정확한 명령, 환경 전제, 기대 출력, 알려진 실패계보, 반증조건 보존.

3. **적대적 시험층**
   - timing, exit semantics, evaluator capture, substitution, 역할반전 가정을 공격하는 reviewer-safe 테스트 추가;
   - 제안 기제가 깨지는 negative result 공개.

4. **주장 원장**
   - 스프린트에서 건드린 공개 주장에 대해 작은 KEEP / MODIFY / KILL / HOLD 계보 공개;
   - 신규성 상태와 과학적 지지상태 분리.

5. **외부 공격**
   - 가능한 경우 별도 검토자·공격자에게 제한된 예산 배정;
   - 프로젝트 내부 재실행을 독립 과학 복제로 표기하지 않음.

6. **종료 판정 메모**
   - 무엇이 강해졌고, 약해졌고, 기각됐고, 미해결인지와 90일 후속을 정당화할 증거를 명시.

### 30일 게이트

자료가 늘었다는 이유만으로 후속자금을 정당화하지 않는다.

다음 중 하나 이상이 발생해야 브리지를 의미 있는 성공으로 본다.

- 검증비용의 실질적 감소;
- 더 강한 시험으로 주장 축소 또는 기각;
