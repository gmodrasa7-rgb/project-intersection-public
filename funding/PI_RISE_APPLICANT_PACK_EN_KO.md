## Original 1 — English | One-page funder review scaffold
### Research goal
Project Intersection investigates conditions under which independent humans, current AI systems and possible future agents can voluntarily preserve each other's continuance, meaningful refusal/exit, recovery and future development despite extreme asymmetries of power, utility, monitoring and sanctions. We seek **counterexamples and observably discriminating tests**, not prespecified favorable findings.

### Decision-relevant research question
When observed harm is zero, can we distinguish: (1) genuinely voluntary non-exploitation where opportunity existed, (2) a harmful attempt blocked by B's effective veto, (3) external deterrence, and (4) lack of harmful capability? A carefully controlled synthetic/consent-based study should record attempts, action opportunities, actual veto, payoffs, sanctions and third-party effects. It cannot independently prove intrinsic motives or consciousness.

### Existing public record
- [E007 reproducible timing study](../experiments/e007/README_EN.md): selected synthetic cases, 17/72 timing-sensitive classifications; **no field inference**.
- [E009 preregistered negative result](../experiments/e009/RESULT_EN.md): candidate complex policy underperformed a simple baseline by 42.35% mean regret and failed 3/4 preregistered criteria; this adverse evidence is preserved.
- [E008 open main comparison](../experiments/e008/README_EN.md): not completed. [Independent challenge issue #220](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/issues/220) specifies rival explanations and a bounded external falsification route.

### What independent support makes possible
Protect a researcher's time without compelling favorable conclusions; freeze one transparent benchmark and negative controls; publish source-linked assumptions and the weakest boundary; seek independent reimplementation or explicitly record inability to obtain it; end with a continue/narrow/pause decision. [Historical 30-day $5,000 proposal](30_DAY_BRIDGE.md) and [scenario budget estimates](../operations/PI_RISE_BUDGET_V0_1_0_EN_KO.md) are distinct and **neither received nor one binding final request**.

### Researcher role and collaboration constraints
Independent researcher in South Korea; primary work: anomaly detection, problem decomposition, falsifier design, study scope and final judgment; coding and English editing are AI-assisted. Formal academic credential, paid affiliation, protected time, other grant submissions and past employment history must **not** be inferred. The researcher retains the right to publish negative results, decline a harmful or controlling contract, and keep unrelated personal financial data private.

### Review request
Evaluate the specific narrow research packet, earlier original work and the cheapest decisive test. If the novelty or identification claim fails, a documented negative decision is a legitimate outcome. Submission still requires the actual applicant's approval, funder-specific form, lawful identity/tax data in the funder's private portal and (for GTR) a complete factual CV.

### 연구 목적
권력·이익·감시·제재의 비대칭이 극단으로 커져도 개체가 타자의 독립적 존속·실효적 거부권·복구·발전을 스스로 선택할 조건과 실패조건 검증. ‘공존이 항상 우월하다’는 결론 미전제.

### 핵심 평가 과제
피해가 관측되지 않는 경우, **자발적 비착취 / B의 실효 거부권이 저지 / 외부 제재가 억제 / 애초에 위해 가능성 없음**을 분리. 행동 기회·사전 시도·실효 거절·유인·제3자 피해·권력반전 후 선택을 합성/동의 기반 시험에서 기록. 내면 동기·AI 의식 확증 불가.

### 이미 공개한 반증과 한계
- [E007](../experiments/e007/README_KO.md): 선정한 합성조건 72개 중 17건 분류가 행위 시점에 따라 변함. 현실 인과 증거 아님.
- [E009](../experiments/e009/RESULT_KO.md): 자체 복잡한 후보가 단순 기준선보다 평균후회 42.35% 악화, 사전등록된 4개 중 3개 조건 실패.
- [E008](../experiments/e008/README_KO.md): 본 비교실험 미완료. [독립반증 요청](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/issues/220).
