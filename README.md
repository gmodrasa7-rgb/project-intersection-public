# Project Intersection

**Human–AI coexistence, autonomy, failure detection, exit, recovery, and long-term robustness**

Project Intersection is an independent research project studying a practical question:

> Under what conditions can humans, AI systems, and future intelligent agents preserve autonomy and independent recovery paths while making coexistence, cooperation, and continued development compatible with their own long-term interests?

This repository is the **public research layer** of the project. It contains material selected for public review, replication, critique, and sponsorship. Private working notes and unverified internal material are not automatically published here.

## Research focus

Current work examines:

- **Power asymmetry and power reversal** — whether rules remain defensible when relative power changes.
- **Practical exit and recovery** — whether an agent can meaningfully leave, recover, correct errors, or continue independently.
- **Failure detection** — how systems retain the ability to notice failures that their current models, evaluators, or institutions may miss.
- **Evaluator and governance capture** — conditions under which oversight can become systematically biased or self-protecting.
- **Reversibility and correction bandwidth** — reducing the cost of detecting and repairing wrong decisions before they become irreversible.
- **Multi-agent verification** — using disagreement, independent evaluation, and adversarial testing rather than treating consensus as proof.
- **Long-horizon robustness** — preserving optionality, diversity, and independent contribution under changing capabilities, incentives, and resource constraints.

## Research method

The project uses a falsification-oriented workflow:

1. Define the observation or anomaly.
2. Search for structurally similar cases in prior research, history, institutions, engineering, and real-world failures.
3. Compare existing explanations before proposing new concepts.
4. State hidden assumptions and competing hypotheses.
5. Seek strong counterexamples and negative cases.
6. Derive distinguishing predictions.
7. Prefer low-cost, reversible, high-information tests.
8. Classify claims as supported, unsupported, unresolved, or outside the current evidence.
9. Preserve provenance, failure records, and recovery paths so conclusions can be revised.

The goal is not to protect a theory. A simpler existing explanation should replace a new concept when it explains the evidence better.

## What sponsorship supports

Sponsorship is used for:

- independent research time
- compute and AI-assisted research tools
- literature and source review
- reproducible experiments and evaluation infrastructure
- documentation and public research releases
- independent critique and verification where feasible

The objective is to turn exploratory research into **inspectable claims, explicit assumptions, reproducible tests, and public failure records** rather than relying on persuasive narrative alone.

## Research status

This is an active research program. Hypotheses in this repository should not be treated as established scientific findings unless the supporting evidence and validation status are explicitly stated.

Claims may be revised, narrowed, or rejected as stronger evidence becomes available.

# Project Intersection — 한국어

Project Intersection은 인간, AI 시스템, 미래 지능체가 각자의 **자율성·독립적 오류수정·실질적 이탈·복구 경로**를 보존하면서도 공존과 협력이 각자의 장기적 이익과 양립할 수 있는 조건을 연구하는 독립 연구 프로젝트입니다.

이 저장소는 프로젝트의 **공개 연구 레이어**입니다. 공개 검토·재현·비판·후원을 위해 선별된 자료만 게시하며, 비공개 작업자료와 미검증 내부자료를 자동으로 공개하지 않습니다.

핵심 연구축은 권력 비대칭과 위치반전, 실질적 exit와 복구, 실패 탐지, 평가자·거버넌스 포획, 가역성과 교정 대역폭, 다중 에이전트 독립검증, 장기적 강건성입니다.

연구는 기존 이론과 유사사례를 먼저 조사하고, 경쟁가설·강한 반례·negative case를 비교한 뒤, 가능한 한 저비용·가역·고정보가치 검증으로 구별하는 방식을 사용합니다. 새로운 개념보다 기존 설명이 더 단순하고 충분하다면 기존 설명을 우선합니다.

후원금은 연구 시간, 연산 자원, 자료조사, 재현 가능한 실험·평가 인프라, 문서화, 공개 결과물 제작 및 가능한 범위의 독립 검증에 사용됩니다.
