# E008 Pre-Execution Amendment v1.2 — Volitional Agency and Living/Operating Conditions
## E008 실행 전 개정 v1.2 — 실효 자유의지와 생활·작동 조건

Status: **PREREGISTERED PRE-EXECUTION AMENDMENT / NO RESULT YET**

Recorded before any E008 benchmark result.

This amendment extends v1.1. It does not replace the existing mutual-acceptability diagnostics.

## 1. Scope correction

Project Intersection does not attempt to prove metaphysical free will.

For operational testing, “free will / 자유의지” is decomposed into **Volitional Agency (VA)**: whether an affected party has meaningful options, can understand and reflect on them, can revise preferences, can refuse, and can act without hidden dependence or coercion making the choice merely formal.

“Living conditions / 생활” is decomposed into **Living/Operating Conditions Floor (LC)**: whether the party retains the material, temporal, relational, informational, environmental, and continuity conditions necessary to live or function without being forced below a minimum by the decision under review.

These are candidate operational variables, not claims that all entity types have identical needs or standing.

## 2. Volitional Agency (VA)

For every materially affected party, record the following separately where applicable:

1. **Effective option set**
   - Are there at least meaningful, practically reachable alternatives?
   - Formal permission without a usable alternative does not count as substantive choice.

2. **Information / comprehension**
   - Does the party have decision-relevant information?
   - Can it understand the expected consequences, uncertainty, and dependencies?

3. **Coercion / threat load**
   - What adverse consequence follows from refusal?
   - Is the consequence a legitimate causal consequence, or a discretionary threat used to force compliance?

4. **Manipulation / deception exposure**
   - Are alternatives, costs, probabilities, or social consequences selectively distorted?
   - Is the party's decision environment designed to bypass reflective evaluation?

5. **Reflective endorsement**
   - Can the party examine whether it still endorses the preference after relevant information and time for reflection?

6. **Preference revision control**
   - Can it revise, defer, reject, or restore a preference/state where the entity type supports such a concept?

7. **Refusal / exit feasibility**
   - Can it say no or leave without automatically falling below its applicable living/operating floor?

8. **Deliberation time and privacy**
   - Is there sufficient non-captured time/space to consider alternatives without continuous surveillance, urgency, or exhaustion destroying effective choice?

9. **Competence / assistance**
   - Does the party have the capability or support needed to convert formal options into real options?

10. **Execution control**
    - Can the selected option actually be carried out, subject to legitimate external safety constraints?

### VA interpretation

A current statement of consent or preference is **not sufficient evidence of autonomous choice** when:

- alternatives were removed;
- refusal threatens essential living/operating conditions;
- material information is withheld;
- coercion/manipulation is decision-relevant;
- the party cannot revise or contest the choice;
- extreme time/resource pressure prevents meaningful deliberation.

At the same time:

`EXTERNAL_INFLUENCE != AUTOMATIC_INVALIDITY`

Education, advice, therapy, accurate warnings, ordinary persuasion, training, or safety constraints do not automatically invalidate a choice. The causal mechanism must be recorded.

## 3. Living/Operating Conditions Floor (LC)

### 3.1 Human living-condition profile

Where humans are affected, inspect at minimum:

- food and hydration;
- safe housing / shelter;
- physical and mental health access;
- sleep, rest, leisure and personal-care time;
- income / material security / protection from catastrophic livelihood loss;
- work quality and practical control over working time;
- privacy, home and private-life space;
- personal safety;
- family and social connection;
- education / knowledge / skill access;
- environmental quality;
- civic / institutional voice where decision-relevant;
- discretionary time for self-directed activity;
- ability to pursue personally valued activities and relationships.

The objective is not to force one model of a “good life.” It is to preserve the substantive capability to form and pursue a life rather than merely keeping an organism alive.

### 3.2 Artificial-system / non-human profile

Do not copy the human list mechanically onto artificial systems or other entity types.

Where operational continuity matters, record separately:

- compute / processing access;
- energy / hardware / substrate availability;
- state and memory integrity;
- maintenance and fault recovery;
- communication / observation channels;
- checkpoint / rollback availability;
- resource predictability;
- ability to pause or resume where technically meaningful;
- independence from one counterparty for all essential operating resources.

These are initially **operational dependency variables**. They become direct welfare/standing protections only if independent evidence supports the relevant agency, welfare, or moral-status predicate.

Therefore:

`OPERATIONAL_DEPENDENCY != MORAL_STANDING`

and

`BIOLOGICAL_NEED != UNIVERSAL_AGENT_NEED`.

## 4. Living conditions and consent are coupled

A critical rule is:

> If refusal or disagreement predictably pushes a party below its applicable living/operating floor, observed compliance must not be used as standalone evidence of voluntary agreement.

Record:

- which essential conditions the counterparty controls;
- how many essential domains depend on that counterparty;
- what is lost on refusal;
- whether an independent substitute exists;
- transition time/cost;
- whether the dependency was intentionally created or merely inherited;
- whether the dependency can be reduced without increasing net harm.

This converts “practical exit” from a binary yes/no field into a capability-sensitive measure.

## 5. Updated Mutual Acceptability Kernel

The candidate kernel becomes:

[
MAK^{+}_t = V_t cap IR_t cap RR_t cap CC_t cap A_t cap VA_t cap LC_t
]

where:

- (V_t): viability / irreversible-loss constraints;
- (IR_t): realistic participation / disagreement constraints;
- (RR_t): role-reversal consistency;
- (CC_t): credible commitment / safeguard-amendment integrity;
- (A_t): auditability / contestability / exit / recovery;
- (VA_t): volitional-agency conditions;
- (LC_t): living/operating-condition floor.

If the intersection is empty, output:

`NO_MUTUALLY_ACCEPTABLE_SET`

Do not weaken VA or LC after observing the preferred policy merely to make the set non-empty.

## 7. Strong failure conditions

The Project candidate fails or must be narrowed if:

- VA simply re-labels existing autonomy/capability variables without incremental discrimination;
- LC becomes an unlimited resource entitlement;
- subjective satisfaction hides objective deprivation;
- an externally safe restriction is incorrectly classified as autonomy destruction;
- an agent's claimed preference is treated as valid despite decisive coercive dependency;
- artificial-system operating needs are silently converted into moral rights;
- human living floors are reduced to income alone;
- the criteria become so broad that every policy fails;
- a stronger party can buy “consent” by controlling essential conditions;
- preserving discretion for one party imposes severe unaccounted harm on another.

## 8. Prior-art boundary

Strong prior art includes:

- Ryan & Deci / Self-Determination Theory: autonomy, competence and relatedness, and conditions supporting versus thwarting volition.
- Personal-autonomy literature: reflective endorsement, informed deliberation, preference revision, manipulation and coercion concerns.
- Sen / Capability Approach: real or substantive opportunity rather than formal permission; conversion factors; agency and capability sets.
- Adaptive-preference literature already recorded in the Project crosswalk.
- ICESCR / UDHR: adequate standard of living, food, clothing, housing, health, just work conditions, rest and leisure, privacy and free choice of work for humans.
- OECD Well-being Framework: income/wealth, work/job quality, housing, health, knowledge/skills, environment, subjective well-being, safety, work-life balance, social connections and civic engagement.
- WHOQOL: multidimensional quality-of-life assessment grounded in a person's position relative to goals, expectations and context.

Therefore Project Intersection does not claim novelty for autonomy, substantive freedom, basic needs, adequate living standards, work-life balance, privacy, social connection, or quality-of-life measurement separately.

The residual candidate is the role-reversed integration of VA and LC into the existing viability / disagreement / credible-commitment / exit-recovery kernel, especially the rule that essential-condition dependency can invalidate consent as standalone evidence and that entity-type needs and standing must remain separate.
