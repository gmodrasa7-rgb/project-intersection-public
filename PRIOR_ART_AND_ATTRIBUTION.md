# Prior Art and Attribution / 선행연구·귀속

Date: 2026-10-04  
Status: **public prior-art boundary**

Project Intersection does not treat a concept as novel merely because it has been renamed in project-specific language. Before a claim is promoted, existing theories, empirical findings, historical cases, and negative results are used as baselines. If prior art explains the claim sufficiently, the project narrows or rejects its novelty.

Project Intersection은 내부 용어로 다시 이름 붙였다는 이유만으로 개념을 신규 발견으로 취급하지 않는다. 새 주장을 승격하기 전에 기존 이론·실증·역사사례·negative result를 먼저 기준선으로 사용하며, 기존 설명으로 충분하면 신규성을 축소하거나 기각한다.

## Current correction

The following broad novelty claims are not retained: selective information permeability as a new standalone theory; independent adoption/rejection as a new standalone construct; dependence/lock-in as inherently equivalent to capture; formal exit as sufficient evidence of material recovery.

The remaining research question is narrower: after established variables such as switching cost, resource dependence, governance, dynamic reconfiguration, and failure correlation are accounted for, do Project-specific operational variables add reproducible held-out information?

## Attribution rule

External authors retain attribution for the concepts and evidence originating in their work. Project Intersection may combine, operationalize, stress-test, or falsify those ideas, but does not relabel prior art as a Project-original discovery. Source uncertainty remains explicit rather than being filled from memory.

### Novelty correction

Broad claims that combine complexity, warning suppression, evaluator dependence, automation burden, reward hacking, shutdown incentives, and power-seeking are **not** retained as Project-original theories merely because they are combined in one narrative.

The remaining candidate is narrower and unresolved: whether Project Intersection's symmetric power-reversal operationalization — including the candidate invariant `AUTONOMY_GAIN <= COUNTERPARTY_AUDIT_EXIT_RECOVERY_GAIN` and repeated benefit/burden divergence tracking — adds reproducible held-out discrimination or prediction beyond established baselines.

Until such incremental value is demonstrated, this remainder is **HOLD / CANDIDATE**, not established novelty.
