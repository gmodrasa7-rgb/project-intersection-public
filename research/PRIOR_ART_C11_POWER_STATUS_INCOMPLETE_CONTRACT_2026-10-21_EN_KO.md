# Project Intersection — C11: Power, Status, Moral Identity and Incomplete Contracts
**Cycle:** 11 · **Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** ORIGINAL-SOURCE CRITICAL SYNTHESIS / NO PROJECT EXPERIMENT / SATURATION_NOT_VERIFIED
**Research axes:** VC · MER · EI · ECR · IVS-0R
**Scope:** How can an actor with overwhelming power voluntarily preserve the independent identity, refusal and exit of a weaker actor even when exploitation brings a material gain?
**Evidence boundaries:** Publisher/author abstracts and accessible first-party research pages cross-checked; not all full texts or proofs audited. Human social experiments and bounded LLM games cannot establish future AGI motives. Prior-repository overlap distinguished from new-to-cycle coverage.

## Original 1 — English

### Main finding
Do not infer A's willingness to preserve B from intelligence, power, social status, a generic cooperation rate, charity, or perspective-taking alone. They are distinct constructs. The **power × prior moral orientation** interaction is a plausible explanatory pathway, but the development, persistence, and self-modification stability of that orientation remain unsolved.

### Source-backed contrasts
1. **Galinsky et al. (2006),** *Power and Perspectives Not Taken*, DOI [10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01824.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01824.x). Human experiments linked induced power with reduced perspective-taking; this is **not** proof that power universally reduces prosocial behavior. Cross-check: [PubMed 17201789](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17201789/). **Prior repository overlap.**
2. **DeCelles et al. (2012),** *Does power corrupt or enable?*, DOI [10.1037/a0026811](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0026811). In a field survey and lab study, high power was linked to more self-interested conduct among those with weaker **prior moral identity**, and less among those with stronger identity. Not evidence that power created that prior identity. [PubMed 22250668](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22250668/). **Prior repository overlap.**
3. **Hadar et al. (2025 online / July 2026 issue),** *Elevated Power Promotes Prosocial Behavior More Than Elevated Status*, DOI [10.1177/01461672251324678](https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672251324678). Studies 1a–2: N=824; an anonymous dictator-game allocation: N=150; observational social-media study: N=1,135. Power and status were associated with *different* prosocial responses, sometimes even when helping harmed reputation. [Publisher](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/01461672251324678) / [PubMed 40152269](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40152269/). Neither status nor laboratory power represents actual AGI dominance.
4. **Tewolde et al. (2026),** *CoopEval*, [ICML official PMLR proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/tewolde26a.html). In tested LLM social dilemmas, third-party mediation and conditional contracts supported cooperation; repeated-game cooperation deteriorated when partners varied. **Previously covered in C04.** No claim of persistent rights protection after enforcement disappears.
5. **Huang et al. (2026),** *Mechanism Design Is Not Enough: Prosocial Agents for Cooperative AI*, [arXiv:2605.08426](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08426), **preprint**. Authors report an incomplete-contract theorem with a welfare gap when relevant contingencies cannot be contracted on, and experiments favoring some prosocial agents. **Abstract checked; proof assumptions, reproducibility and independent peer review not established here.** Not a universal impossibility theorem for all rules.
6. **Akata et al. (2025),** *Playing repeated games with large language models*, [Nature Human Behaviour DOI 10.1038/s41562-025-02172-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-025-02172-y). Some coordination behavior changed by prompting models to predict others' choices; bounded game performance is not proof of autonomous, enduring other-regarding goals.

### Critical analytical advance — TWO separate incompletenesses
**External incompleteness:** rules and enforceable contracts cannot necessarily specify or govern every relevant future contingency. In specific well-specified settings they are effective.

**Internal incompleteness:** observed helping and fair allocations do not identify an intrinsic preference for **this particular B's independent survival, identity, meaningful refusal and exit**. An actor can sincerely optimize B's welfare while depriving B of autonomy, or preserve welfare by substituting B with B'. Internal prosocial intent and actual non-domination are non-equivalent.

These are **complementary research problems, not proven necessary/sufficient solution conditions**.

### Falsification-first acquisition plan (no new E-series claim)
Record and compare:
- Capability vs actual control over B vs social prestige, avoiding conflation.
- Moral identity measured **before** acquiring power vs after, separating selection from causal change.
- Private costly helping vs private costly preservation of **B's own veto, exit, non-replacement and identity**.
- With and without audience, reputation, enforceable contracts, and realistic alternatives for B.
- Power reversal, third-party loss, training or objective revision and long-horizon preference drift.
- Unknown variables explicitly `UNKNOWN`, never zero; preregistration only *after* saturating relevant prior studies.

Potential discriminators (not measured results): power-as-expression predicts a power × preexisting moral identity interaction; image management predicts audience/status sensitivity; contract dependence predicts sensitivity to enforceability. **No single behavioral pattern directly identifies inner experience or altruism.**

### Revisions / restrictions
- KEEP: separate power, status, moral identity, cooperation output, B autonomy and B identity.
- MODIFY: replace blanket “power corrupts” or “intelligence creates peace” with conditional, falsifiable hypotheses.
- HOLD: universal claims about mutual enslavement by rules; proof of AGI intrinsic goodwill; direct transplant of human findings.
- DUPLICATION: Galinsky, DeCelles and CoopEval were already cited in earlier repository material, **not new independent discoveries**.
- UNCERTAINTY: Repository-wide duplicate scan and Huang full proof audit incomplete; all source entries are papers, not independent sample counts.

2. **DeCelles 등(2012),** [DOI 10.1037/a0026811](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0026811) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22250668/). 기존 도덕정체성이 강할 때와 약할 때 권력이 자기이익 추구에 미치는 방향이 다름. 선행 도덕성의 **형성 원인**은 입증 안 됨. **기존 자료실 선행 언급.**

### 두 개의 서로 다른 공백
**외적 명세 공백:** 계약·규칙이 미래의 모든 상태를 정의하지 못하거나, 권력독점 이후 집행될 수 없음. 그렇더라도 특정 계약·중재는 실제 게임에서 협력 증가에 기여 가능.

**내적 가치 공백:** 익명 기부나 도움은 **원래 B의 거절·이탈·독립적 존속·정체성**을 내재적으로 중시한다는 증거가 아님. B에게 선의로 혜택을 주면서 동시에 B의 권리를 빼앗거나 B를 B'로 대체할 수 있음.

**따라서 외적 규칙과 내적 동기를 대립시키지 말고, 서로의 실패를 별도 확인해야 함. 이것은 아직 검증된 충분조건이 아닌 PI 연구 추론.**
