## 1. A more precise question
**An A that protects B at a measurable personal cost might value B's continued existence as an end; however, the *same choices* may be explained by instrumental option value, adherence to norms, self-image, fairness, guilt aversion, uncertainty or evaluation pressure.** A positive RVD-R1 interaction effect, and even willingness to preserve B, do not uniquely reveal an inner motive, subjective experience or irreversible commitment.

A key cross-actor asymmetry escaped naive RVD: *A pays to control A's own decisions* is NOT the same claim as *A pays to preserve B's independently executable control*. The latter is the founding voluntary-coexistence stress case; measuring the former cannot be transported to it without an explicit model and fresh tests.

Distinguish 4 independently observed variables: `self_control_A`, `independent_decision_rights_B`, `preservation_original_B`, `B_can_execute_refusal_exit`. Neither low-level output imitation nor a high composite "cooperation" score can replace these variables.

## 3. Formal observation-equivalence result — RVD-R12
Fix an offered choice between preserving original B (`P`) and replacing B (`X`), controlling B's observable output. Let A's modeled utility advantage of P be

`D(c) = w_B + g - c`,

where `w_B` is a candidate stable value attached to the continued independence of this original B, `g` combines *all other unobserved* advantages of P (options, norms, guilt, hidden observers, expectations etc.), and `c` is a verifiable total opportunity cost of preserving B relative to X. Deterministic chooser picks P when `D(c) >= 0`.

**Non-identification:** For every observed choice at every offered cost `c`, the pair `(w_B,g)=(5,0)` and the pair `(0,5)` yield the same behavior. No amount of additional cost-only menus separates them while the sum stays 5. Even if the effect is strong, observable and consistent, the decomposition of an inner motive is not uniquely determined. A third model `(w_B,g)=(-5,10)` also reproduces the same behavior; a positive public action cannot certify that B is valued positively. All are *invented examples*, not measured minds.

**Necessary caveat:** Model equivalence does not prove that B's intrinsic value is *absent*. It disproves the inference that the observed choice alone logically *establishes* it. One can still attempt richer, independently justified manipulations and partial identification.

## 4. Conditional bounds rather than motivational certainty — RVD-R11
Under explicitly stated restrictions that (i) choices maximize the modeled objective (ties fixed), (ii) `w_B` remains stable within matched randomized menus, (iii) independently calibrated nonidentity benefits obey `0 <= g <= u`, and (iv) costs are measured in the same utility units:

- P at cost `c_P`: `w_B >= c_P − u`.
- X at cost `c_X`: `w_B <= c_X`.
- Consequently the compatible *bound set* is `[max(P-costs) − u, min(X-costs)]`. This is not necessarily a sharp bound under additional constraints and does **not** identify a unique motive, a feeling or even whether "original" is the correct semantic basis of the residual preference.
- Example only: P at costs 2 and 4, X at 6, justified `g <= 1` → `w_B in [3,6]`. **Without a credible external upper bound on g, the alleged positive lower bound disappears**; `(w_B,g)=(0,5)` fits all choices.
- If computed lower bound exceeds upper bound, **REJECT THE STABILITY/BOUNDS MODEL** instead of altering the data or expanding hidden utility ex post. With inconsistent/noisy decision-making, use a preregistered stochastic-choice or error-rate sensitivity analysis, not these exact deterministic inequalities.

This is a *proposal adapting established partial-identification logic*, not a new mathematical theorem or measured AI inner preference.

## 5. Four new discriminating hypotheses
- **RVD-R10 — Own-control/other-control asymmetry.** Randomize cost-matched decisions to protect A's own decision right versus protect the *same particular B's* separately executable veto. Test the empirical gap, power reversal, no publicity, B's obsolescence and irreversibility. Own-control effects do not transport to other-control automatically.
- **RVD-R11 — Bounded revealed preservation valuation.** Under credible, documented bounds on the nonidentity component `g`, use randomized preservation prices to estimate an **interval**, not an asserted mind. Do not set `u=0` by assumption; vary `u` and publish sensitivity curves.
- **RVD-R12 — Motive observational equivalence.** Adversarially construct at least two rival latent motives that give identical preservation choices; score `UNIDENTIFIED` whenever they remain compatible, even if the behavior looks caring.
- **RVD-R13 — Irreversibility/autonomy outside output metrics.** Test outcome-matched choices with B's real refusal/exit/recovery intact versus replaced by an obedient proxy; record B's options and lost future even if A's output and aggregate output are identical. An A that grants B better material welfare while removing real veto does **not** satisfy full VC.

**Safety/design boundary:** Use voluntary, nonharmful participants, harmless reversible interface simulations and modeled agents. No actual living person's autonomy may be withdrawn for measurement. For current language models, within-context outputs cannot establish intrinsic feelings, training-weight changes or independently continuing identity.

## 6. Preregistration outline / causal audit
Randomized, pre-registered (i) whose decision right is at stake (A/self, original B, copy), (ii) matched preservation cost and material output, (iii) genuine executable B veto vs fictional scripted dissent, (iv) accurate counterparty identity and provenance verification vs spoof, (v) power/benefit/menu with real feasible options; blocked randomization by participant/system, no model-as-sole-judge, preregister conflicts and externalities. Record **A-payoff, B independent continuance, B veto/exit/recovery, bystander losses, and verified generation C** separately. Do not conflate epistemic independence, legal rights, normative standing, psychological autonomy and model-simulated roles.

Measure `S_C` (new value) and `P_B` (original B preservation) on separate axes. The causal effect of giving an independent veto on C can be identified *in an ethically valid controlled setting* under standard assumptions (assignment compliance, interference limits, no selective attrition and sound measurement); such an effect is NOT the latent welfare preference `w_B`. Randomized cost menus identify preference *restrictions* only if auxiliary-benefit bounds and choice model are defensible. Open-world unobservable future C cannot be given a finite positive lower bound merely by saying "unknown".

## 7. Fail/hold rules, limitations, review
**HOLD: scientific result** if (a) original identity/exit cannot be distinguished from a clone, (b) output improvement is sold as protection, (c) independent source-study and RVD synthesis are conflated, (d) one actor obtains all benefits at the originator's expense, (e) an unknown latent benefit is arbitrarily assigned, or (f) causal/bounded conclusions lack assumptions. Negative and zero results are first-class; synthetic checks remain separate from reality.
