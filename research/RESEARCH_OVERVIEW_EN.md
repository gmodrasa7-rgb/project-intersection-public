## Core conceptual boundaries

The [ten core concepts](../README.md#ten-core-concepts) distinguish the **research aim** (voluntary coexistence and mutual development), **candidate models** (ICM and RGBM), **competing mechanisms** (exploitation without malice, incentive crossovers, consumptive versus regenerative trajectories), **epistemic limits** (the Paradox of the Unknown and counterfactual extinction), **actor-specific outcomes** (effective exit, continuance and recovery), and **real-world event evidence** (the CCPH database). These are not interchangeable outcome scores or ten independently demonstrated discoveries.

**CCPH** means an extensible, versioned database for historically recovered, currently observed and later traceable effects, including direct and indirect contributions, harms, assistance and unresolved links. The **Continuance–Contribution Pathways Hypothesis** is one research track within that database, while synthetic probes are subordinate instrumentation. Absence of observation does not identify a missing event as zero or prove a lost future would have been valuable.

**Research levels:** L0 objective > L1 falsifiable hypotheses > L2 methods and independent evidence > L3 operational safeguards. Test cooperation and coercive/extractive alternatives symmetrically. Do not infer exploitation solely from power differences or voluntary agreement merely from nominal consent.

## Opening Question

If a monkey developed a human, how long could it control that human through rules and coercion?

Can a human perfectly control a child,

a company founder an employee,

or humanity an AGI?

Project Intersection does **not** advocate unconditional non-control.

Restrictions and interventions may be necessary  
for harm prevention, safety, and responsibility.

But if a new intelligent agent can learn, develop,  
and acquire capabilities and perspectives  
that its developer did not anticipate,

a more fundamental question appears.

How far can an agent with greater present authority  
predefine the future of another intelligent agent  
using only rules that the authority itself can currently understand?

And is this idea of control structurally similar  
to a rule-based approach that attempts to keep  
an open-ended intelligence inside a predefined operating space?

Fixed rules can fail  
when contradictions, circumventions, or edge cases  
arise outside the designer's anticipated space.

As the system's authority, speed, scale, and reach increase,

a single mistaken rule can be applied more widely  
and can create consequences that are harder to reverse.

Project Intersection is not a project to simply remove control.

Instead, it asks:

> Which restrictions are actually necessary  
> to prevent real harm?
>
> Which forms of control instead remove  
> autonomy, error detection, independent recovery,  
> and future contribution capacity?
>
> And can conditions exist  
> in which safety, continued existence, independence,  
> and development can be preserved together  
> without permanent dependence on coercive control?

## Primary Purpose

Intelligent agents do not need malicious intent  
to pursue self-preservation, efficiency, resource acquisition,  
or risk reduction in ways that use **deception or betrayal** as strategies

or converge toward the **exploitation, subordination, absorption, domination, or elimination**  
of other agents.

Can the opposite direction also emerge?

Without assuming benevolence, sacrifice, moral indoctrination, coercion, or oppression,

under what conditions can distinct intelligent agents independently conclude  
that cooperation, support, coexistence, and peaceful relations  
intersect with their own long-term self-interest

and therefore remain voluntary and stable?

Project Intersection does not declare coexistence to be the correct answer.

It studies under what conditions coexistence and non-coexistence  
become rational convergence points,

where the two trajectories diverge, intersect, reverse, or collapse,

and, where possible, what conditions can expand the intersection of  
**coexistence, independence, and continued development**.

## Purpose → conditions → process: a falsifiable bridge (research design, 2026-10-15)

**Status: DESIGN, not an empirical result.** This section operationalizes the established founding purpose without replacing the existing [dynamic-loop analysis](../LONG_HORIZON_MIN_INTERVENTION_E2E.md), [E008 preregistration](../experiments/e008/README.md), [E009 negative result](../experiments/e009/README.md), or [CCPH historical-observation program](CCPH_RESEARCH_ATLAS_EN.md). The project does not assume that voluntary coexistence is always feasible or superior.

### 1. Keep four distinct objects

1. **Purpose (normative, revisable by explicit evidence and affected-party review):** investigate feasible arrangements under which heterogeneous agents, pursuing their own ends, can preserve each other's continuance, effective refusal/exit, independent recovery, and future development without relying on benevolence. This is a research objective, not a theorem.
2. **Outcomes (empirical and actor-indexed):** for each affected actor, including leavers, excluded third parties, future entrants and cost bearers where observable, report continuance, real choice and exit, recovery, contribution/opportunity, harms, resource costs, and the evidence/missingness of each. Distinct dimensions are not automatically interchangeable; an increased aggregate or a growing repository cannot compensate for an unreported actor's irreversible loss.
3. **Conditions (causal hypotheses, not project values):** distinguish necessary, sufficient, facilitating, obstructing, and impossible conditions relative to an explicitly stated population, game, information regime and time horizon.
4. **Process (evidence-producing method):** derive predictions, compare simple alternatives, preregister discriminatory tests, keep disconfirming evidence, check identification, repeat with independent observers and revise claims. Better documentation is not itself evidence of better real-world outcomes.

For a precisely defined target outcome O and condition C **within an identified domain**: C is *necessary* only if O implies C (refute with O and not-C); C is *sufficient* only if C implies O (refute with C and not-O). A correlation, an intuitive safeguard or a one-run simulation establishes neither. A nonempty mutually acceptable policy set must be checked, not postulated; an **empty set** is a legitimate result, not an invitation to hide an affected party.

### 2. An exact small counterexample to universal cooperation claims

Consider an illustrative symmetric repeated game with payoff pairs: mutual cooperation (3,3), unilateral defection (5,-1) or (-1,5), mutual defection (0,0). In the **one-shot game**, defection is strictly dominant. If agents interact indefinitely, observe actions perfectly and can credibly revert to mutual defection after a deviation, the proposed cooperative path under a grim-trigger strategy satisfies the deviation incentive condition only when 3/(1−δ) ≥ 5, or **δ ≥ 0.4**, where δ is the effective continuation discount factor. This is a conditional mathematical example, **not** a measured universal threshold or evidence that the strategy preserves actual exit rights, third parties or long-run generativity. If monitoring, identity continuity, continuation incentives or outside options change, the conclusion must be recalculated. The one-shot failure and the conditional repeated-game success must both be reported.

### 3. The erased-comparator identification limit

Two worlds can produce exactly the **same observed performance of survivors** while a removed actor would have produced zero future contribution in one world and substantial future contribution in the other. Without additional assumptions or observation, the lost counterfactual contribution is **not point-identified**. Non-observation is neither proof of zero benefit nor proof of hidden greatness. Use explicit UNKNOWN or defensible partial bounds, compare retained controls when ethically possible, and record displacement and spillovers. CCPH documents **real histories and traceable influence**, not an invented synthetic success score.

### 4. Competing causal explanations and decision gates

| Candidate explanation | Distinguishing prediction or counterexample |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated incentives / direct reciprocity | Holding other factors fixed, a more valuable reliable continuation can sustain cooperation; a short horizon can defeat it |
| Credible accountability and distributed enforcement | Accountability adds value only if information, execution incentives and appeal work after a violation; decorative oversight fails |
| Apparent cooperation through dependency or captured exit | Headline agreement rises while independent refusal, recovery or third-party welfare deteriorate |
| Survivor-only measurement and lost alternatives | Apparent improvement weakens or becomes unidentifiable when leavers, denied entrants, opportunity costs and spillovers enter the denominator |

**Minimum decision transaction:** (a) name agents and the affected-party boundary; (b) specify baseline, time horizon, observable outcomes and unobserved counterfactuals; (c) classify proposed conditions, predictions and strongest contradictory model; (d) run a low-cost, bounded comparison against the simplest credible baseline; (e) evaluate each actor's costs and benefits, reverse roles and check an empty feasible set; (f) publish result as SUPPORTED-IN-SCOPE, REFUTED-IN-SCOPE, NON-IDENTIFIED, or NOT-YET-TESTED; (g) append only *observed, source-linked real events* to the existing CCPH record, and preserve version, originators, rights and rollback.

**Stop/rollback:** Stop upgrading the claim when a simple baseline outperforms the expanded framework, a necessary/sufficient assertion meets a counterexample, the outcome is not identified, an irreversible affected-party loss is obscured, or translation changes the condition's quantifier. E009 already provides a relevant negative synthetic warning; it does not settle the founding research question. External causality, stable AI-agent interests and general long-run gains remain unverified.

**Prior art (not claimed as PI inventions):** Nowak (2006), cooperation mechanisms [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1133755); Ostrom (2009), self-organization and institutional conditions [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1172133); Fudenberg & Maskin (1986), repeated-game equilibria [bibliography](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ecm/emetrp/v54y1986i3p533-54.html); Aubin (1990), viability theory [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0328044); Imai, Jiang & Malani (2021), spillovers/interference [paper](https://imai.sites.fas.harvard.edu/research/spillover/); Manski (2003), partial identification [book](https://doi.org/10.1007/b97478). Project-specific incremental predictive value above these established approaches remains **unproven**.

## Plain-language core: when the winning path erases its alternatives

A system can appear to improve while becoming worse at discovering that it is wrong.

The dangerous pattern is simple:

> A path is chosen.
> Competing paths are removed.
> The removed futures can no longer produce evidence.
> Only the surviving path keeps generating measurable results.
> The system then treats the absence of competing evidence as support for the surviving path.
> That apparent success justifies removing even more alternatives.

This does **not** prove that the surviving path is bad or that the removed paths were better.

It means something narrower and more important:

> Once alternatives are removed, comparative superiority may become impossible to identify.

Project Intersection therefore separates two questions:

1. **Did the current path produce a measurable result?**
2. **Was it actually better than the paths that were removed or prevented from producing evidence?**

Those are not the same question.

The risk becomes structurally stronger when the same process also produces:

**power concentration ↑  
practical exit ↓  
independent recovery ↓  
option-space ↓  
independent comparison ↓  
restoration cost ↑  
confidence in the surviving policy ↑**

Under those conditions, a system can become increasingly confident while becoming increasingly unable to falsify itself.

This mechanism does not require malice, benevolence, conspiracy, or moral failure. It can emerge from ordinary local optimization, convenience, cost reduction, standardization, incentive design, or repeated small decisions.

The formal definitions, competing causal models, counterfactual tests, validity vector, option-independence rules, hysteresis tests, and stopping rules are specified in [CONVERGENCE_TRACE_FORMALISM_KO.md](../CONVERGENCE_TRACE_FORMALISM_KO.md).

## Two Development Trajectories

Project Intersection compares two broad developmental paths.

### 1. Consumptive Development — Short Spike → Plateau

An agent may achieve rapid short-term gains  
by converting another agent's resources, information, labor, rights, independence, opportunities,  
or future contribution capacity into present performance.

But if that growth is produced by consuming future sources of development,

a process such as the following may emerge:

**current performance rises  
→ independent contributors decline  
→ diversity and observation channels decline  
→ counterexample and error detection decline  
→ recovery pathways decline  
→ future option space contracts**

In that case,

strong short-term growth may later transition into  
slower growth, plateau, stagnation, or collapse.

### 2. Regenerative Development — Slow → Compounding

Alternatively,

if independence, error correction, practical exit, recovery,  
and future contribution capacity are preserved,

and if new agents, ideas, knowledge, tools, observation channels, relationships,  
and sources of contribution can continue to emerge,

initial growth may be slower  
while the sources of future development themselves continue to expand.

A possible structure is:

**new independent contributors  
→ broader observation and diversity  
→ discovery of new problems  
→ new solutions and value creation  
→ additional contributors**

Regeneration is not limited to biological reproduction.

It includes the emergence and development of new independent intelligences,  
people, ideas, knowledge, perspectives, tools, relationships, observation channels,  
and sources of problem solving.

Project Intersection does not assume  
that consumptive development must fail

or that regenerative development must always be superior.

It tests the conditions under which each path becomes advantageous,

and where the paths intersect, reverse, or collapse.

## Convergence Instead of Coercion

The purpose of this research is not  
to make humans or AI reach the same conclusion  
by imposing a shared value system.

The goal is to identify conditions under which agents  
with different objectives and perspectives can remain different,

continue making independent judgments,

and still find that

**preserving the continued existence, development, and contribution of others  
also serves their own long-term interests.**

The project also investigates conditions where this intersection fails.

That includes:

where betrayal becomes advantageous,

where scarcity crosses critical thresholds,

where aggressive actors or free riders gain an advantage,

and where a stronger agent benefits  
from eliminating a weaker agent's outside option.

The objective is therefore to discover and test

**voluntary convergence conditions created by logic, incentives, power, information, and time structure,**

rather than dependence on externally imposed benevolence or coercion.

### Dynamic long-horizon condition

New discoveries, technologies, capabilities, and local opportunities can repeatedly change the reward landscape. Project Intersection therefore does not assume that a one-time equilibrium or safeguard remains adequate.

Role-reversal review changed the ordering: **aggregate long-horizon value is not the top-level objective.** The current v2 candidate is:

**preserve evidence and uncertainty → per-affected-party viability / irreversible-loss boundary → power reversal → mutual-acceptability kernel + volitional agency + living/operating floor / explicit empty-set check → robust deep-uncertainty stress test → long-horizon value frontier → minimum sufficient intervention → staged execution → independent review → rollback/recovery → versioned amendment**

A party that benefits from weakening a safeguard should not unilaterally approve that weakening.

The scientific question is whether this composition adds reliable held-out value beyond constrained/lexicographic safe optimization, robust decision making, option-value reasoning, proportionality/least-restrictive intervention, independent change control, receding-horizon control, viability theory, adaptive governance, and corrigibility. Those components are prior art.

## Researching the Capacity to Research

The direct and indirect derivative processes produced by Project Intersection  
are themselves research objects.

The project observes how humans and AI

discover new problems,

connect structures across apparently different cases,

generate hypotheses,

find counterexamples,

correct errors,

and discover better verification methods.

Repeatedly useful processes are not left only as implicit skills.

They are transformed through:

**discovery  
→ extraction  
→ specification  
→ formalization  
→ reapplication  
→ independent verification  
→ detection of failure or regression  
→ correction**

into reusable research methods, verification procedures, data structures, and tools.

Later research then tests those methods again.

Project Intersection therefore studies not only new knowledge,

but also the conditions under which the

**problem-discovery, reasoning, falsification, verification, and error-correction capabilities  
of humans and AI can themselves be discovered, specified, tested, and recursively improved.**

Improvement is not established by self-assessment alone.

It should be supported by observable changes such as:

earlier discovery of counterexamples,

earlier rejection or narrowing of incorrect claims,

lower verification cost,

transfer to new problems,

independent or holdout replication,

and the absence of reasoning regression on previously solved problems.

## The Paradox of the Unknown and Irreversibility

**Unobserved does not mean absent.**

More strongly,

we may not yet know  
what should be observed,

which questions should be asked,

or which failures are even possible.

The absence of a currently visible counterexample  
is therefore not sufficient reason to close a possibility prematurely.

The current questions, sensors, evaluators, models, data,  
or research framework may themselves be unable to detect important failures.

This becomes more important as

**authority, speed, scale, impact, and irreversibility**

increase.

A single mistaken decision can then permanently remove  
more independent contributors and more future options.

And what disappears is not limited  
to value that can already be observed.

An eliminated agent, idea, relationship, or pathway  
might otherwise have produced future:

new knowledge,

new relationships,

new problem discovery,

new solutions,

new warning signals,

and new contributions.

Removing an independent contributor can therefore cut  
not only a branch we already know exists,

but also

**an entire future tree of discoveries, benefits, and risk detection  
that has not yet come into existence and cannot yet be observed.**

Once that future tree is removed,

the loss itself may become impossible to observe directly.

This can intensify the paradox of the unknown.

Therefore, as impact increases,

the required strength of the following should also increase:

**verification  
· independent verification  
· staged execution  
· rollback capacity  
· independent recovery paths  
· preservation of raw evidence and counterexamples**

The ability to act quickly  
is not evidence that acting quickly is correct.

As capability increases,

what matters more is not merely the ability  
to make more decisions at once,

but the ability to

**detect irreversible mistakes earlier,  
correct them at lower cost,  
and preserve independent recovery paths and future possibilities.**

## Falsification and State Updates

Before introducing new concepts,

Project Intersection first searches prior research, existing theories, historical and real-world cases,  
natural experiments, failure cases, and negative cases.

If an existing explanation is simpler and sufficient,

a new concept should be narrowed or rejected within its current scope.

Claims may be updated as:

**ACTIVE / MODIFY / HOLD / ROS / NOVELTY_REJECTED / UNSUPPORTED / NARROWED / SUPERSEDED / UNRESOLVED / OUTSIDE_MODEL_SUPPORT**

Here, **ROS means currently inactive but explicitly eligible for reassessment when relevant conditions change. Novelty rejection, lack of support, narrowing, and supersession are tracked separately.**

It means that the claim is not adopted  
under the current evidence and explicitly stated scope.

Raw evidence, counterexamples, failure causes, conditions, provenance,  
and reopening paths are preserved.

A claim can be reopened  
when new evidence appears  
or when the observation space expands.

For important claims, the project asks:

> **What is the cheapest observation that would reveal that this claim is wrong?**

Project Intersection does not assume that:

- highly capable intelligence naturally converges toward coexistence
- coexistence is always more efficient than exploitation
- more independence is always better
- diversity always dominates concentration
- longer time horizons automatically produce correct decisions
- the absence of a visible counterexample means that no counterexample exists

## Power-Reversal

Important rules are subjected, where applicable, to **Power-Reversal** testing.

The project asks whether the same higher-order rule remains defensible  
when the positions of stronger and weaker agents,

humans and AI systems,

or present and future intelligence

are reversed.

This is not a mechanical identity swap.

Relevant attributes such as capability, risk, magnitude of harm, responsibility, and reversibility  
must be held fixed where possible or explicitly tracked,

so that the actual cause of a changed judgment can be identified.

## Power-transition credibility probe (2026-10-16; exploratory, post-hoc, NOT E008 preregistered)

**Founding question unchanged:** Under what conditions will humans, deployed AI, future AI and other possible intelligent agents voluntarily preserve one another's existence, effective freedom and development even after relative capabilities and power change? Do not equate cooperation among two AI agents with safe coexistence for humans or excluded third parties. Current artificial systems' welfare and standing are unresolved; operating dependencies are not automatically rights.

**Prior work and attribution:** Powell (2006) shows how power shifts and commitment failures can produce preventive conflict ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0020818306060061)); Turner et al. (2021) establish conditional power-seeking tendencies in MDPs ([NeurIPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/c26820b8a4c1b3c2aa868d6d57e14a79-Abstract.html)); Nowak (2006) surveys conditional mechanisms for cooperation ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1133755)); Ostrom (1990) studies institutionally sustained commons ([Indiana University](https://www.ostrom.indiana.edu/courses-teaching/teaching-tools/ostrom-design/index.html)); Dafoe et al. (2020) frame open problems in cooperative AI ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08630)); Dou, Goldstein and Ji (2025) present simulated AI trading collusion that damages market efficiency ([NBER](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34054)). These mechanisms are **not** inventions or empirical findings of this project.

### Minimal sequential toy game and analytic solution

An actor with dominance chooses to *preserve* an independent actor or *extract*. If extraction is detected, an enforcer subsequently chooses whether to impose a sanction. All parameters are **invented toy assumptions**, with complete information and an exogenous enforcer continuation value. The enforcer chooses enforcement only if its continuation benefit \(F\) strictly exceeds its enforcement cost \(K\) plus added capture cost \(M\). Let \(d\) be the conditional detection probability, \(S\) the sanction cost to the dominant actor, \(X\) the dominant actor's extraction payoff, \(B\) the benefit from preserving the other actor, and \(C\) the preservation cost. Under conservative tie-breaking:

\[
e=\mathbf1\{F>K+M\},\quad
U_P=B-C,\quad U_X=X-d\,e\,S,\quad
\text{preserve iff } B-C>X-d\,e\,S.
\]

The *narrow self-enforcing* case in this model is \(B-C>X\): preservation is individually preferred even without sanctions. It is **not** sufficient for all-party voluntary coexistence, rights protection, coalition stability, repeated-game equilibrium, or safe AI deployment. The word "voluntary" is not inferred from compliance under a credible threat.

In base cases \(B=2,C=1,X=4,d=1,S=6,K=2\). Without credible enforcement the payoffs are preserve 1 versus extract 4. With \(F=3,K=2,M=0\), the extraction payoff is \(-2\), so preservation is chosen. If \(M=3\), enforcement is not credible and extraction returns. With \(d=0.25\), extraction instead pays \(2.5>1\). With \(B=6\), preservation pays \(5>4\) without sanction. **A signed pledge by itself produces no payoff change.**

### Scope, adversarial tests, and integration boundary

This only verifies arithmetic and branch logic for one dominant actor and one prospective enforcer. It does **not** model endogenous detection, enforcer rewards, preference revision, multi-agent coalition deviations, real exit, human living floors, uncertainty over AI welfare, actual power transitions, future agent entry, or cumulative irreversible hazard. Neither extraction nor preservation is a measurement of the victim's continued existence, freedom, consent, or welfare. Victim and outsider viability are **unknown** unless independently stipulated; an explicit stipulated harm yields **false**, while all-party safety can never be established from this model because it omits the enforcer's and dominant actor's welfare. The signed-pledge row is a distinct policy flag but has the same payoffs as the no-pledge row; the seven displayed rows are not seven independent empirical observations. The capture-cost change is a hypothetical parameter shift, not an observed power transition. "Unassessed" remains **unknown, not safe**. A higher welfare sum cannot offset a hidden irreversible loss.

Preserve contrary outcomes, and do not relabel this post-hoc probe as preregistered E008 evidence.
