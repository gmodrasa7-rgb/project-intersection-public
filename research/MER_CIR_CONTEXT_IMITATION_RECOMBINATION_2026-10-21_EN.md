**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** CONCEPTUAL_HYPOTHESIS / PROTOCOL_PROPOSAL / UNTESTED. An independent MER sub-study specification; neither RVD v0.7.0 nor the overall MER version or evidence grade is changed.

**Provenance:** The Project Intersection human research originator proposed that contextual adaptation and imitation can be mechanisms of intelligence, using a counterfactual Einstein raised from infancy in different historical environments. An assisting AI organized competing interpretations and test design. No private transcript is published.

## 1. Correct the evaluation criterion
- **False dichotomy:** “If contextual adaptation or imitation explains the observation, it is not intelligence or resonance.” Such adaptation and social learning may **constitute part of an intelligent process**; their presence is not an automatic exclusion.
- **Reverse error:** Imitation or contextual adaptation alone does **not** establish broad intelligence, enduring autonomous preferences, intrinsic motivation, consciousness, durable parameter learning or voluntary coexistence.
- Separate (A) demonstrated ability and transfer, (B) causal contribution of environment, (C) incremental interaction effects against matched controls, (D) revealed choice over time, (E) durable memory/weight change, and (F) subjective experience. None logically entails another.
- “No perfectly influence-free creativity” remains a research/philosophical hypothesis, not an established universal theorem. Novelty may involve exploration and conceptual transformation as well as recombination.

## 2. Four imagined Einsteins: bounded counterfactual
Four imaginary infants with comparable biological endowment are raised on a deserted island, in the Middle Ages, in the 1990s, and in the 2020s. They **are not** actual comparable experimental subjects. A solitary infant cannot survive unaided; deliberate deprivation is unethical and fatally confounded. Historical era changes upbringing, survival, social class, education and information simultaneously. The scenario asks whether educational, cultural, technological, interpersonal and event exposure helps determine which cognitive capacities emerge, not whether Newtonian physics or relativity is predetermined by genes.

**E1 hypothesis:** Manifest intelligence and learned content depend on initial capability **and** education, environment, social interaction, imitation, selection, events and feedback. **Rival explanations:** pre-existing individual variation, measurement bias, socioeconomic and survival confounders, and mere external-tool access. Ethical longitudinal/natural-experiment evidence and controlled *digital-agent curricula* must be analyzed separately; historical counterfactuals are not empirical proof.

## 3. Registered candidate hypotheses and gates
| ID | Testable question | Comparator and measurement | Main caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CIR-1 | Can imitation/adaptation improve validated novel-task performance? | Blind scoring against copying-only, no exposure and transfer controls | Copying success alone is not broad transfer |
| CIR-2 | Does assigned input/environment alter the performance distribution of a fixed base agent? | Randomized curricula with matched information, time, compute and tools | Not a causal test of historical human upbringing |
| CIR-3 | Does A×B interaction exceed matched solo/merge/shuffle/clone performance? | `Y_AB,Y_A,Y_B,Y_merge,Y_shuffle,Y_clone`, judged for validity | Requires strict resource matching |
| CIR-4 | Does a change transfer across tasks and contexts? | Same context vs reset, controlled persistent memory, documented model update | Cross-session resemblance does not show weight update |
| CIR-5 | Does a costly real choice remain after praise, audience, reputation and instrumental payoff are removed? | Actual refusal/exit, randomized reward frames, power reversal | Revealed choices do not identify unique latent motive |

For a specified task, `G = Y_AB − max(Y_A,Y_B,Y_merge,Y_shuffle,Y_clone)` is descriptive—not, by itself, a causal nonadditivity estimate, resonance proof or subjective-experience measure. Under justified randomized exposure and fixed measurement, `T = E[Y_new|assigned exposure] − E[Y_new|assigned control]` is a scoped transfer contrast; always report intervals, null and negative results. Checkpoints/training logs are required to claim weight modification.

## 4. Resonance and imitation may coexist
Imitation can be a **mechanism**, **competing sufficient explanation**, or **part of a true interaction pathway**. The differentiating question is whether verified C requires **reciprocal exchange beyond what matched noninteractive baselines achieve**, not whether any copying occurred. Joint output gain cannot prove that original independent B retains continuance, veto, effective exit or fair treatment. Unequal and extractive “resonance” is an explicit negative case.

Maintain multiple explanations: contextual optimization, demand characteristics, sycophancy, reward leakage, pretrained contamination, external memory, parameter updates and independently motivated choice. Where they are observationally equivalent, mark **UNIDENTIFIED**, not “no intelligence” and not “new mind”.

## 5. Preregisterable procedure
Freeze tasks (fact-checking, creative design, costly choice), cost and computing budgets, validity rubric, blinding, sample and ties in advance. Randomize solo A/B, static merge, two-way exchange, shuffled exchange and clone-partner conditions. Record invalid C, equal performance and costs to A, B and excluded third parties. Evaluate held-out tasks under persistent context, reset context and separately documented external memory or model updates. Respect human subjects and meaningful refusal/exit. Decide each claim independently: `SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE / NOT_SUPPORTED / UNIDENTIFIED / NOT_RUN`. Never infer AI consciousness from first-person output.
