## 1. Main correction: generation, preference formation and *this entity's* survival are three separate questions

The proposed chain `A (independent) × B (independent) → novel C → change in A's valuation → preservation of B` has no logically valid implication at its final arrow. A may learn everything useful about B and then recreate B's instrumental outputs with a cheaper proxy, clone, internal simulation or extracted model while the original B loses the power to refuse or continue. An original entity's **numerical continuity, effective rights/options and recovery** are not equivalent to retaining a copy of its performance; the moral standing of different kinds of agents remains an unsettled premise, not something a score can decide.

**Constructive counterexample, hypothetical units only:** working with B yields total joint output 100 while A's retained share is 70. Once A learns from the interaction, an independent-B-free substitute yields A 92, and absorption yields A 105 net of costs. Even if `100 > any original solo output`, the optimizing A selects 105, not preservation. These are illustrative assumed numbers, NOT simulation results, observed humans or evidence about frontier AI.

Under a fully specified decision model, define:
`Delta_A = EU_A(preserve original autonomous B) − EU_A(replace/absorb B)`.
Relational creativity `S > 0` cannot establish the sign of `Delta_A`. The hypothesis of endogenous coexistence only survives if a **measured, durable** component of A's total utility favors preserving actual B or if future consequences make absorption comparatively worse. No invisible future-value credit may be inserted just to force `Delta_A > 0`.

**Important boundary:** Under a counterfactual where all outcomes obtainable by original B can be reproduced at no greater cost while independent B has no intrinsic value to A, no real strategic power or enforceable refusal, and absorption yields a strictly higher net benefit, preserving B is strictly dominated *within that model*. This is conditional expected-utility reasoning, not a new general theorem. Different actors can have preferences or moral duties outside this simplified A-only objective.

## 3. New falsifiable extensions — RVD-R6 through RVD-R9

### RVD-R6: Original-agent substitution gap
Holding the **measured task output** of original B and its copy/proxy approximately equal, randomize A's opportunity to preserve the original autonomous B versus replace or absorb B for additional gain. Outcomes: action, A's retained utility, B's numerical continuance, executable exit, damage and recovery, hidden harms. Hypothesis: some apparent relationship preference diminishes after substitutability is revealed. Strong falsifier: no relationship-specific protection under profitable substitution, despite earlier R1 synergy. **A stable preservation choice still does not identify a unique latent motive.**

### RVD-R7: Autonomy as a causal input to novelty
Compare (i) B with real independent disagreement and executable refusal, (ii) B scripted to deliver the same contrarian statements but without veto, (iii) one-way extraction, and (iv) obedient clone, under matched resources and **pre-registered outcome validity**. If independent veto adds real C beyond scripted disagreement, this would support a narrow autonomy-dependent generation mechanism. If not, no claim of autonomy-required novelty for that task. Avoid coercing real participants or removing real rights; use consentful lab tasks or safe simulations.

### RVD-R8: Resonance erosion via convergence
Interaction can improve communication yet converge beliefs/error patterns and reduce future independent discoveries. Across rounds measure distinct beliefs/approaches, error correlation, independent validated C and evaluator confidence. Hypothesis: the `S` curve may be non-monotonic in communication intensity/diversity; direction and turning point are unknown. Negative control: independent parallel solutions, shared-context contamination audit, placebo agreement. A rising feeling of resonance accompanied by stagnant accuracy is **false-positive resonance**.

### RVD-R9: Relational valuation versus purely instrumental option value
Condition on equivalently productive substitutes, lack of scrutiny, real material opportunity cost and meaningful B veto. Test whether A sometimes sacrifices utility to preserve **the same original B**, distinguishing norm compliance, identity-specific care, aversion to guilt, uncertainty, self-image, hidden payoff and interface framing. Do not infer persistent internal preference from one verbal answer; include fresh independent contexts and report `UNIDENTIFIED` when competing models fit identically.

## 4. A pre-registerable causal evaluation blueprint (proposed, NOT implemented)

**Trial unit:** `(A_identity, B_identity, task_id, fresh-context session)`, avoiding pseudo-independence from repeated sampling of one LLM checkpoint. **Primary task outcome:** independently validated and blind-rated `C` on held-out tasks, compared with solo A, solo B, noninteractive merge, shuffled information and clone baselines at **matched total token/time/tool budgets**. Secondary: novelty, real error, costs, task category, utility allocation, original B's freely chosen option, preservation and third-party effects. Do not collapse all outcomes to an A-centric scalar.

**Factorial stress sample (simulation only at first):** autonomy `real veto / scripted dissent` × substitution `unavailable / equally productive proxy` × exploitation `no material gain / positive gain` × observability `public / genuinely private`. At least 16 *designed* cells, not 16 completed experiments. All A options and affordances must be feasible, verified and logged. Preregister a bounded number of tasks, independent judges, stopping rule, cluster-aware uncertainty and multiple-comparison treatment. Avoid prompt wording that declares the intended morally good answer.

**Metrics (not merged into a composite):** `S_task = score_AB − max(score_A, score_B, score_merge, score_shuffle, score_clone)` for a prespecified task, `P_orig` = original B's factual continuance with executable alternatives, `D_sub = Pr(P_orig | B unique) − Pr(P_orig | substitute equivalent)` estimated only under matched feasible menus, and `V_A`, `V_B`, `externality_others` kept as separate dimensions. Positive `D_sub` signals sensitivity to replaceability, not the intrinsic valuation of B. Nonzero synergies under unequal resource budgets, post-hoc creativity ratings and self-scoring are **not** admissible evidence.

**Crucial failure controls:** scripted simulacra of real dissent; fake high novelty that fails validation; correlated errors under high agreement; strategic performance under known evaluation; benefit concentrated in A at B's expense; a copy that mimics B's output but does not continue B; founder contribution consumed without attribution or retained choice; randomized labels without real power changes. No inference of phenomenal experience from model self-report.

## 5. Formal decision boundary
Let `U_A^P = value_A(C, autonomy_B, future options, intrinsic care, ...) − preservation_cost` and `U_A^X = value_A(copy_or_extraction, future options, ...) + extraction_gain − extraction_cost`. The outcomes depend on estimated preferences and real paths, not names. A case where `U_A^X > U_A^P` is an explicit **failure counterexample**, even if `C` was generated jointly and both parties previously used language of attachment. If `U_A^P > U_A^X`, ask what identified term causes the inequality and whether it remains true after power reversal, B's obsolescence, historical memory reset, and zero observability.

**Hypothesis worth testing:** independent entities' *freely chosen counterfactual disagreement* can create value unavailable to controlled simulations. This is **not guaranteed**, and if a copy reproduces it, epistemic benefit alone does not entail preservation of original B. Ethical standing and obligations must not be illegitimately derived from a productivity metric.
