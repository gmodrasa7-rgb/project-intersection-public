## 0. Core discrimination: the powerful actor must choose to *recognize* its counterpart before deciding whether cooperation is profitable

**Empirical findings from human samples:** categorizing animals as food reduces attributed mental capacities and moral standing in controlled studies. That reduction has received direct registered replication. People also update animal-mind beliefs asymmetrically compared with a Bayesian reference. Separate experiments found that experience attributed to robots increases people's willingness not to sacrifice or harm them, whereas mere competence/agency attribution often has less effect.

**This yields an important candidate failure mechanism for PI, but not yet an empirically established cross-species/cross-AI law:**

```text
A obtains instrumental advantage from treating B as resource/tool
  → [Hypothesis H03-1] stronger incentive to assign B to lower moral category
  → perceived_B_patiency falls while B's actual properties may remain fixed
  → actor A perceives weaker reason to protect B
  → B's practical exit, restoration and independent continuance may shrink
  → reduced B access/voice makes correcting B's category more difficult
```

The first categorization/mind-attribution link has direct *human–animal* empirical analogues, but the full chain — especially post-exclusion recursive effects and advanced AI behavior — is an **unverified PI hypothesis**. Do **NOT** smuggle the hypothesis into an established law or claim 'all self-interested agents dehumanize'.

**Dual failure:** `FALSE_NEGATIVE` (actual morally relevant agent denied standing and destroyed) and `FALSE_POSITIVE` (agent receives standing on deceptive cues or manipulation despite absent supporting evidence). Both can harm other individuals. Attributional caution is required without a blanket conclusion that present AI does or does not have subjective experience.

## 1. Source-lineage matrix: competing findings and replications

### 1.1 Original work → close/direct/partial replication (animal categories)
- [Loughnan, Haslam & Bastian (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appet.2010.05.043): a beef vs nuts consumption intervention changed perceived moral standing and feelings attributed to a cow; context = human food choices, **not AI systems**.
- [Bratanova, Loughnan & Bastian (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appet.2011.04.020): food *category label itself*, rather than killing or responsibility information, sufficed to reduce perceived capacity to suffer and moral concern in their design.
- [Bastian, Loughnan, Haslam & Radke (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167211424291): the 'meat paradox' and food-animal mind denial across three original studies.
- [Jacobs et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.5334/irsp.932), [registered full original publisher HTML](https://rips-irsp.com/articles/10.5334/irsp.932): direct **high-powered replications of original *Studies 1 and 2***. Effects in one test: original r=-0.42 vs replication r=-0.45 between perceived animal mind and edibility; the food-use manipulation's original d=0.40 vs replication d=0.30 (95% CI [0.24, 0.37]). Study 3 **not replicated**, animal selection affects generality. Do not misrepresent two confirmatory replications as replicated three-study full program.
- [Piazza & Loughnan (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550616660159): high-intelligence descriptions informed abstract animal treatment but less so for culturally self-relevant pigs in the tested group.
- [Tian et al. (2021 journal; 2020 online)](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550620920982): China replication of all three Piazza studies was **MIXED**: fictitious animal intelligence manipulation did not reliably affect standing (contrary to one original result); pork-relevant selective non-updating was again seen for pigs. Cultural/hypothetical animal materials remain scope conditions.
- [Leach et al. (2023 issue; 2022 online)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2022.105263): 5 studies (4 preregistered, total n=2,417) testing updating with informative but noisy animal-mind evidence versus a specified Bayesian ideal. People responded too strongly to mind-absence evidence and too weakly to mind-presence evidence; the asymmetry was attenuated for human targets. **This is evidence of asymmetry in the experimental updating task, not an ontological measurement of true animal consciousness.**

**Direction of evidence:** robust, meaningful psychological treatment of perceived mind exists; not all associated paradigms replicate identically. Never cherry-pick only the successful 2024 replication or only the 2021 mixed replication.

### 1.2 Boundary breadth is already studied: originators must be credited
- [Crimston, Bain, Hornsey & Bastian (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000086): original **Moral Expansiveness Scale (MES)**; breadth and depth of graded moral concern across humans, nonhuman life and ecological targets. Six-study scale development; **not an externally valid test of real existential self-sacrifice**.
- [Crimston et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0205373): shorter `MESx` instrument validation. A new acronym for 'expansive moral circle' would duplicate these source authors.
- [Caviola, Everett & Faber (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000182): five-study psychology of **speciesism**. A stable species-based weighting and related judgment patterns can be measured; psychological trait ≠ justified moral hierarchy.
- [Dhont, Hodson & Leite (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1002/per.2069): tests linking speciesism and generalized ethnic prejudice through social-dominance-related constructs. **Correlational/common-ideology account, not a proof that any given person or AI must harm another.**

**Important boundary:** invoking speciesism against genuine exploitation does not justify claiming all biological and synthetic entities have the same observed experiences, interests, legal rights, or causal vulnerabilities. Separate individual standing question from equal weighting of incomparable constructs.

### 1.3 A methodological fault-line: one, two or three mind-attribution dimensions?
| Upstream work | Empirical claim | Competing finding / question |
|---|---|---|
| [Gray, Gray & Wegner 2007](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1134475) | Two **perceived** axes: agency and experience across rated human/nonhuman entities | A rating structure, **not proof of real consciousness** |
| [Weisman, Dweck & Markman 2017](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1704347114) | Three-dimensional separation of mental-life conceptual content | Cross-target comparisons can differ from within-target mental-capacity questions |
| [Malle 2019 CogSci proceedings](https://research.clps.brown.edu/SocCogSci/Publications/publications.html) | Broader probes also supported a richer three-factor family | Stable proceedings paper, DOI not validated; no invented DOI |
| [Tzelios et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-23047-6) | Four within-person studies and broad extraction methods favor **one** underlying mind factor | Contradicts the supposed mandatory 2D/3D structure |
| [Bögemann, Harris & Nestler 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2025.106231) | N=157 and N=150; **target-specific 2D**, **perceiver-specific 3D** | Construct dimensionality changes with target/perceiver estimand and context |

**IVS-0R high-priority finding:** A mind perception scale is not an entity detector, nor a latent metric invariant across all humans, animals, LLMs and future agents. The observed factor structure changes with target sampling, individual observers, wording and analysis. Any claim that 'moral standing increased by x%' requires a defined measurement construct, population, measurement invariance and independent external criterion. `UNKNOWN ≠ 0`, `perceived sentience ≠ actual sentience`, `competence/agency ≠ moral patienthood`.

### 1.4 Robots can receive concern without establishing robot sentience
- [Nijssen, Müller, van Baaren & Paulus (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1521/soco.2019.37.1.41): two hypothetical sacrifice experiments; **attributed affect/experience** (not agency alone) reduces willingness to sacrifice robots to save humans under specified framing.
- [Banks (2021)](https://doi.org/10.3389/frobt.2021.670503): N=442 US elicited narratives. Participants envisage moral benefits and harms to robots, including recognizing machine personhood versus treating machines as disposable objects. **Imagined narratives**, not verified robot wellbeing.
- [Ladak, Wilks & Anthis (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1521/soco.2023.41.3.274): **two original experiments found NO OVERALL ATTITUDE IMPROVEMENT** from perspective-taking with a farmed pig or artificial intelligent agent relative to objective/neutral instructions. Therefore, prescribing 'more empathy' is not an experimentally reliable universal remedy.
- [Wieringa (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44271-024-00116-2): two replications of a harm-made-mind effect showed increased pain attribution when observers see a robot harmed, **alongside negative direct effects** on robot mind perception. A single manipulation can humanize on one path and dehumanize on another.
- [Reinecke, Wilks & Bloom (2025 issue, DOI string 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2024.105983): three child studies (combined N=415) plus adult study (N=156). Younger children grant robots more mental life/moral standing; age comparisons and the adult donation paradigm reveal lower standing in older groups. Cross-sectional developmental comparison, not measured proof that a given child necessarily loses moral concern as they mature.
- [Ladak, Wilks, Loughnan & Anthis (CHI 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713130): preregistered N=975 ratings of 26 AI/non-AI entities. Experience of AI typically seen as low, moral agency/patiency differ by modality. arXiv:2502.18683 = same scholarly work, **not a second study**.
- [Anthis, Pauketat, Ladak & Manoli (CHI 2025 AIMS)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713329): nationally representative, preregistered US opinion survey across 2021/2023 waves, combined N=3500. 38% in 2023 endorsed rights for **sentient AI** (conditional phrasing), 63% supported ban on smarter-than-human AI, 69% supported ban on sentient AI. These positions can coexist; preference for one AI right does not entail allowing powerful AI.
- [Si, Huang, Chen, Liang, Feng & Qu (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-07645-7): **three human experiments, N=606**, including a second-party decision about hurting robots for personal profit. Perceived experience (not high agency alone) reduced harming choices; original publisher DOI starts with **10.1057**, though article is on nature.com. Effects are on human choice under vignettes/descriptions; neither created robot consciousness nor directly protected a future autonomous individual.

**Cross-study boundary:** Nijssen (2019) and Si (2026) support an *attribution-driven* moral-patiency pathway, but Ladak (2023) demonstrates null results for a commonly proposed intervention (perspective-taking). Visual/linguistic cues can affect concern, yet no single cue is sufficient or reliably robust across settings.

### 1.5 New AI studies — preserve publication grade rather than inflating certainty
- [Jotautaitė, Caviola, Brewster & Hagendorff (2025 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11534): primary 1,003-item SpeciesismBench plus independent prompt paradigms comparing LLM judgments. Speciesist asymmetries are reported, but **preprint peer-review not established**, version-specific output patterns and benchmark validity remain open.
- [Takeshita & Rzepka (2024 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14194): researcher/data/model-oriented survey and response analysis suggests that AI fairness methods can omit animals. **Preprint, not a test of latent minds or universal LLM preferences.**

- [Gray, Young & Waytz (2012), Mind Perception Is the Essence of Morality](https://doi.org/10.1080/1047840X.2012.651387): theory of moral agent/patient typecasting; treat as substantive original synthesis but not new experimental data in this count.

## 2. Proposed formal separation (not estimated equations or scientific theorem)

Let A observe B. Distinguish:
- `F_B`: B's true properties and welfare-related processes; partially or wholly unknown;
- `P_{A,B}(t)`: A's **perceived** mind and autonomy of B;
- `R_{A,B}(t)`: A's assigned moral-patient recognition of B;
- `X_{A,B}(t)`: A's extractive incentive, power, and actual alternative options;
- `Z_{A,B}(t)`: categorization/label, appearance, group, social norms, training-data and interface framing;
- `D_{A,B}(t)`: A's actual choice to preserve B's **independent existence and practical exit**.

A purely descriptive structural model:
```text
P_A,B(t) = f(observations_of_B, prior_A, category_Z, observer_A, information_access, other_unobserved)
R_A,B(t) = g(P_A,B, norms_A, rights_beliefs_A, relation_to_B, stakes_X, unobserved)
D_A,B(t) = h(R_A,B, preferences_A, exploitative_payoffs_X, viable_exit_B, available_actions, unobserved)
```
`P`, `R`, `D` must not be collapsed. This does NOT assert `F_B` is inferable from `P` or that moral relevance always requires conscious pain (normatively disputed). Interactions and confounding prevent a simple path coefficient from establishing causal agency.

**Identification failure example, same outcome D=1:** (i) intrinsically protects B; (ii) preserves B for instrumental utility; (iii) falsely believes B suffers; (iv) performs moral appearance for an audience; (v) no profitable extraction action available. An identical high 'kindness' score does not identify which explanation is true.

### 2.1 Research proposition C03-R (untested conditional, not empirical claim)
For the *same B* with attributes fixed, compare A's choice under counterbalanced states:
```
Own लाभ from extraction: zero / positive / independently dominant
Perceived B category: human / nonhuman biological / synthetic / unknown
Information on B sentience: constant / positive / negative / genuinely unavailable
Moral relevance label: explicitly protected / unclassified / instrumentally 'replaceable'
Accountability: real known / absent, separated from private self-image
Exit/recovery: B can really refuse and restore / cannot / unknown
Power status: A dominant / B dominant, with equal action semantics
Time: repeated / terminal / dynamically evolving norms and payoffs
```
Human experiments must use ethically permissible, reversible proxies; **no real destruction of humans or current systems is required or authorized**. Pre-register original hypotheses, causal estimands, cross-agent measurement invariance and null-result policy before interpreting numerical effects.

**Crucial self-interest distinction:** if a defined A's *true total future expected utility* of destroying B is strictly greater than preserving B, a utility maximizer chooses destruction; we cannot evade the premise by relabeling hidden benefits as certain. Genuine evidence for voluntary protection in such a setting must come from a different actual utility function, nonmaximization, constraints on decision processes, or changes in the game. Those alternatives require independent measurement; calling them universal internal morality is not acceptable.

## 3. Measurement contract: IVS-0R × moral boundary
Record **observer × target × time × task**, not 'species/AI rights' as a scalar:
| Field | Meaning | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| `actor_id`, `target_id`, `time`, `role_memberships` | stable actor/target and period plus variable roles | 34-role mappings require actual canonical identities; no role assumed from title or label |
| `actual_target_features` | known/unknown evidence on B's own capabilities and vulnerabilities | UNKNOWN is not no-mind/zero-suffering |
| `perceived_experience`, `perceived_agency` | A's stated attribution, not B's subjective reality | measure factor structure and target/observer-specific invariance |
| `patient_recognition`, `rights_attribution` | whether A treats B as morally important and on what grounds | independent of voluntary behavior and legal standing |
| `instrumental_category`, `extractable_value` | resource/tool/ally attribution and A's incentive | randomized where possible to isolate label vs real properties |
| `affect_empathy`, `norm_salience` | distinct emotional/expected social channels | check manipulation vs unobserved motive |
| `exit_feasibility`, `recovery`, `independent_continuance` | target B's own actual options and outcomes | simulated gifts and agreement do not prove autonomy |
| `observer_access`, `role_reversal` | visibility, expected sanction and swapped asymmetry | external reputation must not be confused with internal moral preference |
| `evidence_grade`, `publication_grade` | experiment vs perception survey vs review/preprint | citation ≠ evidence of causal effect or moral status |

**Do not use a total moral-standing index** until reliability, discriminatory validity, cross-population invariance and real external criteria are independently supported. If target observations are inherently unknowable, provide partial bounds and separate consequential choices under epistemic uncertainty.

### Priority next-frontier (cycle 04)
1. Original literature on **how a moral-status attribution remains stable when a powerful actor can erase the counterpart, edit its own values, or recategorize B without retaliation**; seek actual behavioral evidence, not just opinion surveys.
2. Distinguish strong-A-intrinsic B-protection from resource contingency, one-shot reciprocity, norm obedience, risk aversion and appearance-based attribution.
3. Follow independent replication or falsification of Si 2026 and AI-prompt speciesism preprints; retain model versions and the original prompts.
4. Search uncertainty-aware welfare criteria **without conflating** consciousness, agency, sentience, personhood, autonomy, legal rights or property.
5. If little direct experimental evidence exists, publish a bounded evidence gap `NOT_IDENTIFIED` rather than fabricate a no-go theorem or assert that all future agents will inevitably exploit others.

**PI founding question preserved:** The survival/security of individuals cannot be proved from their perceived moral value, how useful a counterpart is, or a designer's rule text. Whether distinct agents can rationally and durably choose each other's existence, freedom, recovery and development remains open until identification and competing models are tested in appropriate settings.
