# ECR–CMP: Compassion, Inhibition, Power and Voluntary Coexistence
## Mechanism-discovery atlas, competing hypotheses and falsification protocol

**Original 1 — English · 2026-10-18 · IVS-0R candidate v0.1**
**Status: DESIGN / NOT EMPIRICALLY VALIDATED / NOVELTY UNRESOLVED.**
**Original 2:** [Korean edition](ECR_CMP_MECHANISMS_2026-10-18_KO.md). **Protocol:** [EN](../experiments/ecr_cmp_v0_1/README_EN.md), [JSON hypothesis atlas](../experiments/ecr_cmp_v0_1/mechanisms.json), [audit code](../experiments/ecr_cmp_v0_1/audit.py).

## Abstract
ECR is a proposed actor–constituency–relation program to identify concrete affected actors, measure observed actions and consequences, compare explanatory processes, predict conditional behavior, and discover conditions for voluntary long-run coexistence despite power reversal. The founder's recent question adds compassion, conspecific harm aversion, predation inhibition, innate versus learned mechanisms and their effect on cooperative development. Merely scoring compassion or supplying synthetic utilities cannot answer it. We introduce a typed mechanism collection (not a personality rating), a limited behavioral cost-threshold bound, an observational-equivalence counterexample and an experiment protocol that independently varies opportunity, dependence, sanctions, target scope, power, rule-modifiability and third-party harm. The objective is to distinguish, not assume, compassion-like concern, opportunism, reciprocity, coercive compliance, cultural norms and technical inhibition. This work has no actual person/AI data, real AGI forecasting or established novel theorem.

## 1. Founding question and preserved corrections
The research goal is to learn when distinct self-interested or differently motivated humans, AI and future agents can prefer preserving each other's existence, independent judgment, refusal, recovery and development over domination, exploitation, forced absorption and destruction, even when relative power changes. Love, compassion, peace, independent freedom and mutual flourishing are research-relevant values, not disposable variables. The question is not whether cooperation is always optimal.

Originator-specified roles A–S and Y0/Y1, and B01/C01 indexing, are preserved. T–AG are optional AI-proposed supplementary categories, not founder-owned codes. A is an initially vacant hypothetical highest-power role, not a measured person or AI. Identity, actor kind, role, group and behavior are distinct. A human may occupy several roles without becoming several victims. New characteristics do not alter the 34 proposed role indices.

Do not erase earlier evidence: E007 changed 17/72 classifications in selected synthetic conditions; E008 main policy comparison not executed; E009 complex Project-v3 mean regret 42.35% worse than best simple comparator, failing 3 of 4 criteria; E010 post-hoc exploratory; draft ECR v1.4 PR #211 had only synthetic logic tests; role draft PR #197 remains unmerged. ## 3. Separate phenomenon, mechanism, implementation and evidence
**What is observed:** actual opportunity, target, ability, action, loss avoided, third-party effects, consent, exit, effective authority and time.
**Candidate proximate process:** recognition of distress; affective resonance; other-oriented concern; damage aversion; disgust; kin affinity; attachment; reciprocity; inequity aversion; reputational gain; normative compliance; sanction avoidance; tactical deception; self-protective cooperation.
**Origin/implementation:** genetic/developmental influence, biological structures, AI hardware/architecture, software rule, learned weights, training, culture, contract, organization and resource ecology. DNA/HW/SW/LRN/ENV are not mutually independent additive contributions.
**Evidence:** original observations, instrument and construct, affected individual/collective, time, measurement error, counterhypothesis, and independent validation. Synthetic observations cannot certify inner experience.

The earlier five-element compassion taxonomy is a research starting point, not five established independent factors. During the founder-preferred collection stage retain separate candidate variables: suffering recognition, perspective inference, emotional resonance, tolerance, other-oriented motivation, action, willingness to bear a cost, violence inhibition, cost threshold, social distance, group/kin/age/species/future scope, attention allocation, training/developmental changes, power sensitivity, dependency sensitivity, punishment sensitivity, rule reversibility, self-modification and external harm. Do NOT compress them into one CMP score without factor and predictive validation.

## 4. Five observationally indistinguishable policies
Consider hypothetical B03 protecting B02 while harm is impossible or costly and both are allies. Identical observed protection may come from:
- P0: cannot extract because opportunity is absent;
- P1: extracts only when sanction vanishes;
- P2: extracts when no longer dependent on B02;
- P3: protects only selected in-group members;
- P4: protects regardless of group, observation or dependency, **within the specified tested policy**;
- P5: obeys a technically enforced harm-blocking rule while the rule is operational.

The same baseline trace is consistent with all six. Changing feasible opportunity, sanction, ongoing dependence, target membership, visibility, power and rule state may separate some of them. Even a finite battery does not uniquely identify subjective feelings or behavior in all future environments. A no-opportunity zero-attack count is not voluntary restraint; penalty-backed nonattack is not necessarily voluntary; an apparent generic helper can participate in collusive external harm.

## 5. A narrow behavioral bound — not personality scoring
Within an explicit two-action model, protected target harm h>0, private protection cost c>0 and fixed other-regard parameter w≥0, assume **no sanctions, reciprocity, publicity, different resources, or unmodeled motives**:
  U(protect)−U(abstain)=w h−c.
Under a declared weak tie convention, protect suggests w≥c/h and abstain suggests w≤c/h. Several opportunities yield the set [max protected c/h, min abstained c/h] in **that specified model and regime only**. If lower>upper the stable-single-weight model is rejected, not the actor, compassion or moral worth. No valid opportunity gives [0,+infinity), not w=0. A range does NOT prove felt compassion. This method is a simple revealed-preference construction, not newly discovered psychometrics; controlled incentive confounds must be ruled out rather than scored away.

## 6. Inference and prediction protocol
Compare held-out prediction of precisely defined actor-specific events:
M0 opportunity-aware frequency baseline;
M1 observed action + power/target/period graph;
M2 M1 plus latent alternative mechanism hypotheses with uncertainty.
Freeze identity splits, the exposure denominator, third-party harm floors, task horizon and label rules before looking at outcomes. Use log/Brier scores for declared forecast targets, calibration, subgroup-specific harm misses, scope coverage, negative controls, and stress tests after power transfer. Missing targets are not known-safe; abstention must be reported. Stronger mechanism language requires independent contrasts/interventions, not simply a better classifier.

**Discriminating test menu:** independently vary (a) ability/opportunity for extraction, (b) sanction, (c) ongoing resource interdependence, (d) target similarity/competitor/in-group/out-group, (e) visibility and evaluator incentives, (f) effective power/control, (g) ability to alter software or oversight, (h) outsider harm and coalition deviations, (i) duration/recovery/exit. For actual humans, use consented non-harmful anonymous allocation tasks, no coercion or personal covert scoring.

## 7. Competing research hypotheses, not presumed truths
H-CMP-01: specified functional concern reduces predatory actions under matched costs and opportunity. Rival: reciprocal gain/deterrence.
H-SCOPE-02: care and inhibition vary with social/species/time scope. Rival: task misunderstanding, exposure bias.
H-POWER-03: earlier behavior fails to generalize after concentrated power. Rival: changed rewards, unavailable counterfactual.
H-BIO-04: developmental/genetic, learned-cultural and technical constraints have distinguishable robustness signatures. Rival: no unique signature after controlling environment.
H-COAL-05: internal solidarity may facilitate exploitation of outsiders. Rival: verified third-party floor maintained.
H-OPTION-06: preserved independent actors generate future options, warnings, correction and cooperative innovation. Rival: preservation cost exceeds uncertain benefits.
H-NONCOERCE-07: cooperation persists after external sanctions remove and effective exit remains; but mere persistence does not prove subjective compassion. Rival: unmodeled longer-term incentives.
H-REVERSAL-08: a role-exchange invariant protocol exposes double standards that depend on target identity, not verified harm difference.

Most important **negative explanations** to preserve: altruism can be exploited; favored-group compassion can harm outsiders; rigid prohibitions can prevent legitimate rescue; compassionless agents can cooperate instrumentally; unknown future contributions need not be valuable; concentrated power with perfect substitution and no intrinsic regard or external checks may render voluntary preservation suboptimal; model predictions alter incentives themselves.

## 8. Prioritized operational plan
P0: preserve originator distinctions, negative E009 finding, frozen scenario definitions, and correct absence-of-opportunity denominators.
P1: collect the full hypothesis-mechanism inventory without artificially treating proposals as observations.
P2: test six countermodel policies with safe opportunity/sanction/dependence/scope contrasts, reject unsupported causal labels and impossible one-number score inference.
P3: construct matched experimental conditions and independent annotations to validate measurement; avoid human person-ranking.
P4: benchmark M0/M1/M2 with prospective out-of-sample tasks, power changes and explicit coalition outsider losses.
P5: only if actual incremental performance improves, integrate into ECR predictor and coexistence condition analysis; retain failed hypotheses with reconsideration triggers.

**Falsification first:** if only ability/opportunity changes explain nonviolence, revise CMP explanation. If repeated preference choices yield contradictory cost ratios, reject the stable-weight model. If apparent care disappears after dependency is removed, the instrumental explanation remains viable. If team cooperation harms unlisted parties, fail global coexistence. If a complex model loses to M0, preserve the negative result.

## 9. Provenance, harms and conclusion
This is a founder-question-led, AI-assisted synthetic research protocol, not a validated theory of human evolution, feelings or future superintelligence. Avoid psychological profiling from group status or DNA, unauthorized human surveillance, hidden social worth rankings, compulsory modification, and discrimination. Different actors' legal rights/agency and unknown AI moral status should be examined separately without deciding them by role code.

The testable value of ECR lies not in labeling love or malice but in discovering **which combinations of concern, inhibition, knowledge, reciprocal incentives, independent options, and power structures actually generate voluntary, non-predatory coexistence**. A truthful result may say some conditions fail. The ECR program must remain capable of registering this failure and finding a better structure, rather than replacing the founding question with control-harness compliance.
