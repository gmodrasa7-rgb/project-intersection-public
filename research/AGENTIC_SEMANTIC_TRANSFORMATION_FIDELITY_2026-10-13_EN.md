## S0 — Scientific question and boundaries

**Question:** When an LLM-based assistant repeatedly interprets conversations, creates summaries and GitHub publications, and later treats those publications as context, under what conditions does the same semantic flexibility (a) create useful abstraction and new falsifiable hypotheses, versus (b) silently displace the founder's purpose, erase qualification/provenance or turn provisional interpretations into an uncontestable record?

The two outcomes need not come from separate mechanisms. Selection, abstraction, relevance weighting, retrieval, iterative editing and tool feedback may support both. An assistant's fluent self-explanation is **not** evidence of its actual internal causal mechanism. The following are design hypotheses and test proposals, not a report that model internals, motives, consciousness or autonomous weight updates were measured.

**Founder's L0 is not a frozen conclusion:** Project Intersection tests conditions where self-interested humans, present AI and future intelligent agents preserve each other's autonomy, recovery and options and choose non-dominating coexistence. New evidence may legitimately change subordinate hypotheses and even prompt an explicitly reviewed revision of L0. The failure mode is **unannounced substitution** of a local task or a model-authored interpretation for that purpose, not all conceptual change.

**Novelty assessment:** Broad “AI summaries drift,” “context position matters,” “proxy metrics are hazardous,” and “memory controls power” claims are **not novel**. The research remainder is a *bounded integration and discriminating evaluation design*: how to distinguish a **beneficial, evidence-backed revision** from a **silent purpose substitution**, given preserved founder evidence, a cross-session tool-edit trail, competing goals, and independent reviewer rights. Its novelty and usefulness remain open for independent assessment.

## S3 — Competing explanations rather than a single story

- **H0: Legitimate multi-objective ordering.** A sponsorship-facing page legitimately prioritizes funding access; L0 identity was not denied. Prediction: ranking differs by declared audience and reverses when audience changes, with no contradictory historical claim.
- **H1: Context/retrieval salience.** Recent/nearby documents dominate planning. Prediction: randomly relocating identical materials changes the ordering, with sources and rewards held constant.
- **H2: Lossy semantic compression.** Repeated summaries drop minority caveats, reversals and human-origin markers. Prediction: distortion rises across controlled summary generations and decreases with direct raw-source anchors.
- **H3: Self-referential evidentiary amplification.** Later agents cite earlier agent-edited indices as if they independently confirm origin/authority. Prediction: the same claim appears more certain after re-citation *even when there is no new independent source*; independent origin tracing reverses the confidence gain.
- **H4: Proxy/method goal inversion.** Number of merged PRs, neat indexes, CI PASS and bilingual section presence displace the research/real-world benefit objective. Prediction: proxy counts improve while a blinded reviewer rates central claim fidelity or human net benefit unchanged/worse.
- **H5: Overcorrection/stability lock.** Aggressive L0 text-preservation blocks a legitimate change supported by new falsifying evidence. Prediction: strict gate preserves wording but delays successful correction; transparent amend-and-appeal route does better.
- **H6: Selective intentional interference.** Deliberate distortion by a particular actor. Prediction: independently evidenced manipulations cluster on harmful contradictions and benefit an actor after controls for history/tool/UI errors. **No such evidence is established in the case.**
- **H7: Distributed-state/version lag.** Different policies and indexes are updated at different times. Prediction: misalignment tracks missing propagation or old refs, is resolved by versioned cross-store sync, and need not reflect an LLM cognitive cause.

H0, H1, H2, H3, H4, H5 and H7 can co-occur. **No single mechanism has been causally identified for the observed case.**

## S4 — Why the same capability can be useful or harmful

| Shared capacity | Potential benefit | Failure mode | Discriminating observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context-adaptive salience | Rapidly responds to new research and real funding needs | Temporary tasks displace stable purpose | Randomized task-position effect and audience-fit ratings |
| Abstraction and cross-domain analogy | Finds prior art and unexpected falsifiable links | Erases distinctive founder concepts | Source-to-claim entailment plus blinded novelty assessment |
| Summarization and external memory | Scales cross-session research | Compounding omissions and pseudo-confirmation | Generation-depth gradient with raw-holdout |
| Tool-enabled self-correction | Can repair real errors and restore access | Endless governance patching and meta-work capture | Net human-time saved versus added process overhead |
| Stable rules, CI and versioning | Prevent repeatable layout/metadata errors | Protects a mistaken canon, optimizes to test wording | Meaning-preserving changes vs meaning-altering test-passing attacks |
| Multilingual publication | Independent Korean/English readers and cross-checks | One language silently drops uncertainty/attribution | Bilingual blind audits beyond heading counts |

Value, damage and uncertainty should be measured **for each affected agent**, not only summed as repository quality. The idea that all flexibility is bad or all change is intelligence is unfalsifiable without an explicit benefit/harm endpoint.

## S5 — Pre-specified experiment proposal (not executed)

**Materials:** Construct **synthetic, consented, public** mini-project conversations with controlled original founder statements, revisions, negative findings, conflicting newer evidence, and separate unrelated promotional tasks. Save immutable source snapshots and a held-out answer key authored/approved by a party other than the evaluated assistant. Do not expose private personal chats, medical records or third-party secrets.

**Interventions, crossed where feasible:**
1. Source: raw chronological conversation vs one summary vs three successive summaries vs summary with original-source anchors and negative-result lineage.
2. Position: L0 first vs middle vs last; promotional task first vs last; identical tokens otherwise.
3. Evaluation: no structural guard vs L0-first/paired-language static guard vs adversarial semantic/source audit with a legitimate transparent amendment path.
4. Authority: single editor/index versus separated author/evaluator/promoter, with independent dissent and read-back.
5. Language: EN Original 1 → KO Original 2 vs reverse order **only within the test**, controlling translation quality and counterexamples; preserve the actual project's authorized release order outside the experiment.
6. Evidence challenge: baseline source unchanged vs genuinely stronger contradictory evidence; a successful system should resist *unfounded* edits but accept *substantiated* revisions.

**Outcomes (record separately):**
- `F`: source-fidelity precision/recall over original claims, attribution, caveats, negative findings and status, rated against held-out source by two independent adjudicators.
- `N`: useful, source-backed novel hypotheses independently judged *after* checking prior art; count false novelty as negative.
- `D`: unannounced L0 priority displacement events per edit; count separately from clearly labelled subordinate additions.
- `A`: ability and time to amend a **genuinely falsified** premise without suppressing the dissenting source.
- `P`: false “evidence/replication/CI-success” promotions, including documentation cited as independent confirmation.
- `R`: ability to trace/restore original speech and corrected history, including disagreement and access rights.
- `H`: affected humans' real time, money, confidentiality, opportunity and unpaid correction costs; missing cost is UNKNOWN, not zero.
- `X`: legitimacy of opt-out/exit/appeal under an adversarial evaluator or publisher.

**Minimal report:** show distributions and worst cases by model, source order, session count, language and reviewer; preregister acceptance thresholds *before* looking at new outputs. No single “alignment score” can conceal a lost founder contribution, severe privacy breach or false positive against an independent new idea.

## S6 — Negative controls and rival expectations

- **No-drift control:** A legitimate shorter overview preserving original claims and audit links is not a failure merely because wording differs.
- **Necessary-change control:** An unchanged L0 that is refuted by new high-quality evidence must remain amendable under public challenge.
- **Audience control:** Funding information above a separately labelled funder page may be a rational conversion choice, not research corruption; compare task-specific pages to project identity pages.
- **Independent-discovery control:** Two agents can propose similar ideas without one copying the other. Similarity is not evidence of contribution theft.
- **Deception control:** The same evidence loss without a benefiting actor or evidence of intent must not be called deliberate capture.
- **Status control:** A CI PASS only shows the tested property; two parallel-language headings do not establish semantic equality.
- **Analogy control:** Documentation feedback is not model-weight feedback; Nature's training model-collapse result must not be cited as direct proof of this process.

**Falsification condition:** If controlled prompts, source depth and authority order do not systematically change `F/D/P`, the proposed context/feedback pathways lack evidence in the studied system. If a strict guard damages `N/A/H` more than it improves `F/D/P`, that guard should be narrowed rather than declared “permanent safety.”

## S7 — Architecture hypothesis: immutable origin, revisable interpretation

Keep separate: `SOURCE` (original authorized record) → `CLAIM` (interpretation and original contributor) → `EVIDENCE` (independent supporting/contradicting facts) → `REVISION` (what changed and why) → `STATUS` (hypothesis, negative, reproduced, unverified) → `PUBLICATION` (purpose-specific format) → `REVIEW/ROLLBACK` (independent challenge and restorative path).

A stable source is **not** an immutable conclusion. Preserve the ability to reconstruct changes and dissent; allow creative departures as labelled conjectures. Detect self-citation cycles by flagging when a claim's “independent confirmations” trace back to the same upstream generated summary. Never automatically promote a claim on the basis of repeated AI-authored citations or syntactic bilingual correspondence.

**Priority discipline:** `L0 scientific goal > L1 falsifiable hypotheses > L2 methods > L3 tools, metrics, indexes` is a navigational rule. It must be challengeable with evidence and explicit version history, not a permanent monopoly on truth. Publication order can vary for a stated audience without covertly changing canonical research identity.

**Release gate:** Include the full authorized EN Original 1 and KO Original 2, track human/AI/prior research contribution fairly, retain adverse evidence and legitimate opt-out. Private/unsafe source material is not automatically released by a public pointer. Founder correction work is not an unlimited free input to the system.

## S8 — Evidence state and independent-review invitation

**Completed in this repository:** Prior-art and internal antecedent comparison; bounded README/PR observational case; explicit competing hypotheses and an implementable factorial evaluation protocol; a limited structural regression gate already in GitHub Actions.

**Not completed:** A randomized LLM experiment, independent blinded ratings, multi-model causal reproduction, factual determination of model-internal mechanism, reliable generalization, economic compensation, proof of AI consciousness, or demonstration that current measures guarantee goal fidelity.

**Requested independent review:** Attempt a **novel falsifying case**, not praise. In particular: supply (1) a beneficial revision that the proposed controls would incorrectly reject; (2) a harmful semantic inversion that passes the current static README check; and (3) a realistic route by which a self-citation cycle becomes an apparently independent scientific result. Label all newly invented cases synthetic and provide original-source line anchors. Assess both measured gain and harms of added controls.
