## Original 1 — English

### Distinct concepts

**Accumulation** is the increase in an actor's resource or effective decision right over time. **Concentration** is a rising share controlled by a smaller subset of a specified population. **Convergence** is the growing dependence of different decision or resource pathways on a shared gatekeeper, veto coalition or infrastructure node. **Flow** is a time-stamped, directional transfer, issuance, delegation, conversion or loss of a specified resource or right.

These phenomena must not be conflated: wealth can be dispersed while compute access converges; concentrated ownership need not entail concentrated independent decision rights. Convergence is not inevitable and does not mean one universal ruler.

### Candidate causal pathways

P0 pre-existing inequalities (inheritance, capital, institutions, luck) → P1 cumulative advantage and reinvestment → P2 shared control bottlenecks across capital, data, compute, model goals, evaluation and deployment → P3 optional lock-in or control capture → P4 AI-mediated amplification or diffusion → P5 branching toward entrenchment, fragmentation, replacement or voluntary cooperation.

Every arrow is **conditional** and may reverse. Institutional competition, antitrust, interoperability, open access, independent audit, economic diversification and effective exit can break or redirect the sequence.

### Minimal formal specification

A time-indexed, directed, multilayer graph `G(t)` contains actors, joint-control coalitions and separately typed resource/decision edges. For an additive resource type `d`, balance accounting is:

`X(i,d,t+1) = X(i,d,t) + In(i,d,t) - Out(i,d,t) + Created(i,d,t) - Lost(i,d,t)`.

This is an identity under defined boundaries and consistent units, not a discovered causal law. Joint veto rights, coercive power, information and monetary assets cannot simply be added or exchanged. Any cross-domain conversion needs an independently evidenced mechanism.

**Convergence indicator (proposal):** For a predeclared set of essential tasks, trace all credible independent completion routes and record which share depends on a named indispensable controller or coalition. Publish the denominator, substitutes, uncertainty and legal versus effective control. This indicator alone does not prove exploitation.

### Disconfirmable hypotheses

| ID | Claim to test | Evidence against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PACD-H1 | Some existing resource advantages increase future access to AI control rights. | Entry and governance diffuse after baseline controls. |
| PACD-H2 | Separate power domains can converge onto common essential gateways. | Independent substitutes increase and common dependencies decline. |
| PACD-H3 | In some settings AI increases accumulation feedback rates. | Matched observations find zero, negative or diffuse effects. |
| PACD-H4 | Weak effective exit plus shared gatekeeping expands opportunities for exploitation without requiring universal malice. | Voluntary freedom and independently enforceable protection persist despite dependency. |
| PACD-H5 | Interoperability, real exit, accountability and internalizing third-party costs can interrupt control capture. | Entrenchment remains advantageous despite credible interventions and power reversal. |
| PACD-H6 | Concentrated resources can sometimes support public protection or efficiency without extraction. | Observed exclusion, retaliation or failed remedy disconfirms that local benign interpretation. |

**Hardest test:** Would a stronger self-interested agent preserve another's real independence when exploitation is profitable and there is no detection, punishment or reputational consequence? Measure actual, not nominal, refusal and exit.

### Evidence and prior-art starting set

1. Merton (1968), *Matthew Effect in Science*: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.159.3810.56 — scientific recognition/resource accumulation.
2. DiPrete & Eirich (2006), *Cumulative Advantage as a Mechanism for Inequality*: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.soc.32.061604.123127 — multiple unequal-advantage mechanisms.
3. Barabási & Albert (1999), *Emergence of Scaling in Random Networks*: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.286.5439.509 — preferential attachment in a defined model.
4. Acemoglu & Robinson (2008), *Persistence of Power, Elites, and Institutions*: https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.98.1.267 — formal and informal power may diverge.
5. World Inequality Report 2026: https://wir2026.wid.world/insight/executive-summary/ — estimated global wealth top 10% ~75%, bottom 50% ~2%; this does NOT identify individuals' motives.
6. OECD (2026), *Artificial Intelligence markets*: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/artificial-intelligence-markets_d531d73f-en/full-report.html — concentration risks and countervailing competitive dynamism.
7. Broido & Clauset (2019), *Scale-free networks are rare*: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-08746-5 — counterexample to universal network power-law assumptions.

This is an initial **non-saturated** source set, not evidence that PACD is a novel empirical discovery, a law, or a single fate of all AI systems. The relative influence of inheritance, institutions and personal disposition is not identified here.

## 원본2 — 한국어

### 목적·정의

이미 축적된 인간 사회의 자본·제도·정보·권력이 AI의 목표·보상·평가·배포권과 결합할 때, 권력의 **축적·이동·수렴·분산·복구**를 추적하는 연구축.

- **축적:** 특정 개체가 보유하거나 실질적으로 행사 가능한 자원·권한이 시간에 따라 증가함.
- **집중:** 정해진 모집단의 전체 자원·권한 중 소수 비중이 증가함.
- **수렴:** 서로 다른 권력 경로가 동일한 필수 관문·거부권자·연합에 의존하게 됨.
- **흐름:** 자원·권한이 언제 누구에게 이전·위임·창출·회수·전환·분산되는지 방향을 기록함.

부의 집중과 AI 통제권의 수렴은 별개의 관측. 비대칭을 곧바로 착취로 판단하거나 높은 집중도를 특정 인간의 악의 증거로 사용하지 않음.

### 상태 경로

`기존 불균형 → 누적 우위 → 다층 관문 수렴 → 선택적 포획·잠금 → AI에 의한 강화 또는 분산 → 고착·분열·교체·협력으로 분기`.

이 순서는 필연적 역사법칙이 아님. 신규 진입, 경쟁, 상호운용, 독립적 감사·거부권·실질적 이탈이 각 단계에 개입할 수 있음.
