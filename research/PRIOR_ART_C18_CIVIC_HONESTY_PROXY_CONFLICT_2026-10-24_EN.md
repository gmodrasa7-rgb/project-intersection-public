# C18 — Primary-source audit of civic honesty field experiments and conflicting observational proxies

- Investigation date: 2026-10-24 KST; follow-up observations: 2026-10-25 KST.
- Research axes: VC · EI · MER · ECR · IVS-0R.
- Status: **SEARCH_ACTIVE / SATURATION_NOT_VERIFIED / PI_CLAIM_HOLD**.
- [Korean source edition](PRIOR_ART_C18_CIVIC_HONESTY_PROXY_CONFLICT_2026-10-24_KO.md). This English edition was added on 2026-10-26; independent semantic review is **NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED**. It translates the existing dated investigation, rather than claiming a new literature search or empirical replication on the translation date.
- Question: Do actors protect others when detection and sanctions are low and exploitation is materially advantageous? Can the observed behavior identify voluntary endogenous motivation and the other actor's effective freedom and continuance?
- Method recorded in the source: cross-check journal articles/author PDFs, independent follow-ups, rebuttals and replies, and public data routes. Topic/author strings were checked against five existing public ledgers; repository-wide DOI deduplication across every PR and historical revision was not completed.

## Substantive judgment of the initial round

**Email contact, actual wallet storage/recovery, prevention of property loss, voluntary motivation, and another actor's independent continuance are different observable variables.** Do not collapse them into one honesty or coexistence rate. Preserve both the finding that wallets containing more money prompted more contact and the disagreement over contact versus recovery measures and their interpretation.

## PI application — an observation contract, not an executed experiment

1. Separate cash amounts, gains and others' losses from detection/sanction probabilities, third-party observation and institutional responsibilities.
2. Measure contact, recovery, preservation, effective refusal, exit, recovery capability and continuance of the same B separately.
3. Keep observational equivalence among altruism, self-image, norms, retaliation avoidance, reputation and professional duties unresolved.
4. Preserve counterexamples: contact without recovery; safe storage without contact; protection without exit rights; return because of professional duty; identical behavior with different latent motives.
5. Separate data, observation and interpretation under C10's A/B/C identifiability categories. Without observations supporting AI/superintelligence extrapolation or an extreme absence of sanctions, retain current non-identification.

### Scholarly record #10 — adverse intervention effects and field observations

- Hung, Yiu, Wu, Liu, Wang & Han (2025), *Randomized studies in China show that studying and promoting civic honesty needs to consider local norms*, Scientific Reports 15, 3710. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-87804-z
- **Candidate independent lineage #7**: separate team, recruitment and experiments from the six data-producing lineages above. Do not count the preliminary survey, two online experiments and one field experiment as four independent replications.
- Preregistration: AsPredicted #106827, 2022-09-12, https://aspredicted.org/3cnv-4vy9.pdf (before field collection). Author data/code/codebook: ResearchBox #2291, https://researchbox.org/2291 ; Zenodo preservation DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15040071 ; declared license CC BY 4.0.
- Design: **660 wallets without cash** delivered to institutions in Beijing and Shanghai; randomized control, civic-responsibility, altruism and safety-assurance messages. WeChat QR scans, phone contact and messages were separate observations. Hypothetical online return intentions differ from actual field behavior.
- Phone contact: control **104/164=63.4146%**; civic responsibility **106/162=65.4321%** (+2.02 percentage points, Pearson p≈0.704); altruism **102/167=61.0778%** (−2.34 points, p≈0.661); safety assurance **78/165=47.2727%** (−16.14 points, Pearson χ²=8.6705, p≈0.003234). The publication reports safety-assurance χ²=8.59, p=0.003: this is **approximate reproduction** using a simple 2×2 test, not an exact rerun of the same analysis specification.
- Preregistered **QR scans**: control **116/164=70.73%**, safety **89/165=53.94%** (−16.79 points, simple Pearson χ²≈9.876, p≈0.001675). Phone contact and scans are different outcomes; actual return, owner benefit, rights and voluntary motivation were not measured.
- **Negative result:** the reassurance message reduced contact and QR scans; civic-responsibility/altruism messages did not significantly raise field phone-contact rates. Do not infer intention or mechanism. Cohn's 2019 China email figure of 7% and the 2025 WeChat figure of 59.3% are **not directly comparable effects**: wallet contents, channel, period, sample and design differ; the 2025 authors also warn against direct comparison.

### IVS-0R distinctions: preregistration conflict and interpretation risk

1. **Observed intervention-wording difference:** the 2022-09-12 preregistered safety condition describes a WeChat-verified/authenticated reassuring QR program; the publication describes possible police reporting of fraud, QR safety and a lost-property-center contact. **These are not identical texts.** Actual field stickers, all versions and change dates require further author/supplement cross-checking. Do not describe the result as an exact effect of the intervention as preregistered.
2. **Preregistered analysis priority:** QR scanning was listed first; calls/messages were also preregistered. The article emphasizes the 47.3% phone-contact adverse result and notes a similar QR direction. Both outcomes were recalculated from the ZIP, but multiple comparisons, prior-analysis specifications and timing changes remain unconfirmed pending a complete audit.
3. **2023 collectivism coefficient reassessment:** the Tannenbaum–Maréchal–Cohn article, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2313586120 , reports a table with N=434: changing only the reference city changes the rice-farming percentage coefficient from +0.456 to −1.989; removing city fixed effects changes it to +0.097 (not significant). The reply, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2316228120 , argues that provincial aggregation prevents perfect collinearity. However, when province is determined by city, `X_i = sum_c X_province(c) * 1[city_i=c]`; it is a linear combination of the city fixed effects. **The separate collectivism coefficient in that conventional fixed-effects specification is not identifiable.** Andrew Gelman (2024-05-16) also discusses the error: https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/2024/05/16/a-bizarre-failure-in-the-review-process-at-pnas/ . This is a **rank problem in that specification**, not invalidation of all 2023 wallet observations, proof that the actual cultural causal effect is zero, or identification of motivation.
4. The 2025 experiment is **not** an independent reproduction of the 2023 disputed collectivism coefficient. Its randomized interventions and platform-comparability issue form a heterogeneous follow-up lineage within C18.
