## 1. Forcing B to exercise self-control can itself frustrate B's agency
The usual operational outcome "B directly chooses" is a poor proxy for B's **authorship of the decision-making arrangement**. A competent B may, for deliberative burden, responsibility, risk management, or freely chosen relationship reasons, want to **delegate** a bounded decision without losing the ability to decline an unauthorized decision. When A says "You must choose yourself, because autonomy is always good", A may impose paternalism about agency itself. The opposite "I know your best interest, so I will choose for you" likewise usurps B's prerogative.

Track at least:
1. The **first-order action** B would make (substantive choice).
2. B's **meta-choice** about the legitimate decision maker: SELF or informed SCOPED_DELEGATION.
3. The **scope, informed character, effective refusal, safeguards and revocability terms** of the delegated authority.
4. A's **voluntary action in the face of alternative private gains**: tracks B's meta-choice or substitutes A's preferred controller.
5. A's **capability to arbitrarily override or retaliate later**, regardless of its immediately benign behavior.
6. A's **latent internal reason** for doing (4), not identifiable from its behavior alone.

An A respecting B's expressed wishes under genuinely valid consent can recommend allowing B to choose directly *or* permitting B to delegate. Consent and rights limits precede any mathematical score; never assume unrestricted revocability of legally or ethically regulated delegated decisions.

## 2. Observed restraint does not prove non-domination or intrinsic care
[Pettit's original non-domination account](https://doi.org/10.1093/0198296428.003.0003) distinguishes actual noninterference from retaining a capacity for arbitrary interference. Thus if A agrees today to respect B yet can unilaterally invalidate the agreement tomorrow, those are **three distinct evaluands**:
- `R` = observed, optional voluntary deference to B's rightful choice of decision-maker in a particular case.
- `D` = structural risk from A's discretionary power over B's continued rights, resources and exit, even without current interference.
- `M` = endogenous motivational valuation of this particular original B, competing with norms, reputation, A's idealized view of welfare, self-image and future options.

A's R-positive observed choice is compatible with high D and either strong or absent latent B-specific concern. A protective gate that makes D lower may reduce harm but **cannot prove M**. Pettit's normative account is prior scholarship, not a new Project Intersection impossibility theorem. The strong autonomous A that voluntarily refuses to dominate B must not be inferred from third-party coercion that removes A's capacity.

## 3. Original-author contradictions and heterogeneous own-choice valuation
- [Neri & Rommeswinkel (2017)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2485107) report **aversion to another actor's interference** as dominant among their own-decision-right treatments; this is an experimental working paper.
- [Ferreira, Hanaki & Tarroux (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geb.2019.10.008), in France/Japan laboratory treatments, find **self-reliance** significant while independence and power do not appear significant in their stated decomposition. Their introduction explicitly warns the two studies use **different methods** and that non-significance in their tasks is not a universal absence of independence motives.
- [Feldhaus, Lingens, Löschel & Zunker (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroecorev.2025.105254), European Economic Review, estimate the intrinsic value of **participants' own** choice at an average **8.9% of their endowments** within their incentivized online experimental design. This is *not* a calibrated parameter for A caring about another B, not universally constant, and authors say subject data are available on request.
- [Freundt, Herz & Kopp (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpubeco.2026.105681), Journal of Public Economics, document heterogeneity in own autonomy valuation, including substantial willingness **not** to make the decision personally, conditional on their method's exclusion of instrumental value.
- [Ertac, Gumren & Gurdal (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joep.2019.102200) distinguish the value of personal autonomy and the burden/authority of deciding for other people.
- [Frankfurt (1971)](https://doi.org/10.2307/2024717) distinguishes first- and second-order volitions concerning the agent's will; this is **philosophical antecedent**, NOT experimental evidence about outsourcing to another actor or future AI personhood.

These are source/publisher abstract and selected introduction-level checks, **not** a saturated review or new systematic meta-analysis. Own decision rights are not interchangeable with a stronger A valuing the freedom of an **original other B**.

## 4. Measurement design to actually discriminate a target without endangering B
The [structured pre-registration DRAFT](MER_EI_META_CONSENT_COMPETING_MECHANISMS_PROTOCOL_2026-10-22.json) outlines the smallest ethically permissible human analogue, **not a conducted experiment**. An informed independent adult B privately records a real preference for **SELF** versus **scoped, truly voluntary delegation**, verified for comprehension and with legally permitted opt-out. An A is randomly assigned to a consenting B and is independently randomized to receive authentic disclosure of B's meta-choice or not, and to a permissible small **private profit of overriding** the recommendation. A chooses a proposed decision-control allocation; B still has the final authorization to prevent genuine coercion.

Scientific distinctions:
- Randomized **disclosure** identifies the effect of learning B's authentic preference on A's *recommendation*, conditional on clear assumptions.
- Randomized **private gains** identify a change in the willingness to recommend respecting B's known meta-choice in this bounded arrangement.
- B's initial SELF versus DELEGATE preference is **not randomized**, so differences by type cannot be read as the causal effect of meta-preference itself.
- The recorded A recommendation is **not unconstrained domination behavior**, precisely because B's authentic final authorization must remain protected. Do not generalize an off-path expression to high-stakes real control.
- Neither trial identifies an exclusively intrinsic moral preference because self-image, social norms, forecasted B welfare and future option value remain rival latent incentives.

Seven preregisterable competing accounts: **respecting B's meta-choice; mandatory-direct-choice paternalism; projecting A's concept of welfare; desire for authority; impression/norm motives; instrumental future partnership; willingness to relinquish arbitrary dominating power**. Predicted signs are conditional, not new human experiments or mind-detection rules.

## 5. Robustness and ethical fail-closed criteria
Non-negotiable gate: protected human B with independently given specific informed consent, reversible low-stakes procedures, a viable no-penalty exit, comprehension, independent authentication and no real threat or coerced transfer of inalienable/legal/safety-critical rights. Keep actual resource ownership and unilateral revocation capacity in ECR records. B's apparent meta-choice may itself have been manipulated by A; this must be audited independently. No one may knowingly expose an actual vulnerable B to unilateral coercion merely to measure a powerful A's "true" motives.

Record `NOT_RUN`, `PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED`, `INTRINSIC_MOTIVE_UNIDENTIFIED`, `B_PREFERENCE_COERCED`, `ACTUAL_EXIT_MISSING`, `UNDERPOWERED` and `NOT_REAL_AI_IDENTITY` instead of auto-promoting favorable outcomes.

## 6. Why the Project Intersection question remains open
The hard EI/MER target is not automatically maximizing the number of choices B makes or forcibly producing a favorable outcome for B. It is: **When A can gain from imposing its own will, why and when does A choose to protect the particular original B's legitimately held authorship of its own decision-making arrangement, even as the relationship and power structure change?**

This release is *original-source-guided causal design* and separate measurement contracts, not an empirical demonstration, new RVD theorem, increased literature saturation, or a reason to upgrade canonical RVD v0.7.0.
