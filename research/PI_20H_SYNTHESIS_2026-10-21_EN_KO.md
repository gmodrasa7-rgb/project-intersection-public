### New *conditional deduction* H20-09 — lower bound, not a measurement of a mind
For a bounded **hypothetical** choice, A can obtain known additional gain g ≥ 0 by overriding original B's rights, but instead *freely chooses* to preserve them. Represent all modeled reasons for this choice as rB + u + t ≥ g, with rB ≥ 0 the hypothesized value of **this B's own freedom**, 0 ≤ u ≤ U the combined instrumental/sanction motive, and 0 ≤ t ≤ T all *other* non-B-specific motives including self-image. Under **complete additive payoff modeling**, externally justified upper bounds U and T, stable known g, truly feasible exploitation, independent B rights, and no suppressed terms:

**rB ≥ max(0, g − U − T).**

This follows by rearrangement, **not by a real experiment**. If U or T is unknown or unbounded, return **UNIDENTIFIED**, never zero and never a positive bound. Even setting the observable sanction channel to zero is insufficient when self-image remains unrestricted. Synthetic examples (same modeled observed protection): (g,U,T) = (2,0.4,0.2) implies conditional minimum 1.4; (2,1,1.5) implies 0; (2,0,UNKNOWN) is UNIDENTIFIED. The numerical coefficients are fabricated pedagogical inputs; they are not estimated for any human or AI.

**Crucial falsifiers:** A lacks actual exploitation authority (the observed protection is externally enforced); B's effective veto/exit is absent; t hides other motives; g excludes future incentives; bounds are asserted without independent evidence; original B is silently substituted by another beneficiary; the model omits a strategically relevant observer. In any such case the purported B-specific bound must be **HOLD/INVALID**, even when the chosen action appears generous. Conversely, latent-motive nonidentification alone cannot prove that lasting voluntary coexistence is impossible. This combines, but does not supersede, [PI-RAMP 0.6](../protocols/PI_RAMP_V0_6_EN_KO.md) and [RVD 0.7](RVD_CYCLE07_EFFECTIVE_REFUSAL_EXIT_2026-10-21_EN.md).

1. **Source:** independent original provenance, contrary data, DOI identity, population, non-overlapping sample and saturation status.
2. **Entity/rights:** same B before/after; actual available actions; effective veto, exit, no retaliation and third-party effects.
3. **Choice/motives:** exploitive alternative truly available; all relevant opportunity gains; externally evidenced bounds on non-B-specific explanations; causal intervention and sensitivity.
4. **Transfer/reproduction:** preregistration, genuine independent implementation, changed objectives/power/observers, hostile open-world alternatives, reproducible negative results.

A failure or unknown at any gate must not be aggregated away by an average score. The research target remains voluntary self-interested preservation of independent continuance, not maximal compliance.

### 2. 신규 조건부 추론 H20-09 — 타자존중 동기의 하한 식별
원개체 B의 실질 거부·이탈권을 침해하면 A가 추가 이득 g를 얻지만 A가 자유롭게 보호를 선택한 **가상 결정모형**을 가정.

- rB ≥ 0: B 자신의 자유를 보존하는 데 A가 부여한 가치라는 *가설 변수*.
- u ∈ [0,U]: 제재·관계·평판·전략적 미래이득 등 도구적 이유.
- t ∈ [0,T]: 자기이미지·규범 유지 등 **B 자체를 위한 가치가 아닌** 나머지 이유.
- 선택조건: rB + u + t ≥ g, g ≥ 0.

따라서, **완전히 지정된 가산모형·실제 착취 가능성·g의 정확한 관측·U/T의 독립적 정당화·누락 원인 부재**라는 강한 전제하에서만:

**rB ≥ max(0, g − U − T).**

이는 C14의 제재 설명과 PI-RAMP v0.6의 자기이미지 반례를 결합한 **조건부 부등식 유도**. 사람·AI의 실제 마음을 측정한 결과 아님.

| 가상 입력 (g,U,T) | 논리적 하한 | 해석 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| (2,0.4,0.2) | 1.4 | 강한 전제 내에서만 B특정 가치 양수 |
| (2,1,1.5) | 0 | 똑같이 보호해도 B특정 동기 양수 입증 불가 |
| (2,0,미측정) | UNKNOWN | 외부 제재가 없어도 자기이미지 등 미관측이면 **하한 식별 불가** |

**결정적 반례:** 착취 선택지가 실제 차단돼 있으면 관측된 보호는 자발적 선택이 아님. B의 원개체가 제3자로 대체돼도 모형 점수가 같아질 수 있음. U/T가 수사적 가정일 뿐 실제 상한이 아니거나, 실험자·평판·미래 재평가를 관측하지 못하면 양의 하한 판정 무효. B 거부권 보호와 B에 대한 내재적 선호는 **둘 다 별도 증거 필요**. 마음의 미식별성을 공존 불가능으로 확대하는 것 역시 오류.
