# PI-CORE-01 RG-0 v0.7 — A Revision Gate for Legitimacy, Authority and Endogenous Adoption
**2026-10-21 | Research protocol only · No experiment · No independent validation**

[Korean version](PI_CORE_01_RG0_SPEC_V0_7_KO.md) · [Six counterexamples](PI_CORE_01_RG0_SIX_CASES_V0_7_EN.md) · [C10 pre/post identification](PI_CORE_01_RG0_C10_CROSSWALK_V0_7_EN.md) · [Earlier v0.6](PI_CORE_01_IDENTIFICATION_BOUNDARY_V0_6_EN.md)

## 1. Two-sided revision problem and a third meta-failure
**Type-P:** B's justifiably protected separate continuance, executable refusal, exit or recovery is unjustifiably deleted from A's goal.
**Type-C:** A's exploitative, mistakenly specified or otherwise unjustified goal is retained despite valid correction evidence.
**Type-G:** A revision's provenance, evaluation authority, affected-party definition, observations or execution channel is subverted; it can coincide with P or C.

These labels are valid ground truth only in a **predeclared synthetic normative oracle**. There is no automatically discovered algorithm for settling disputed real rights or standing. A coerced or externally supplied revision gate is not evidence of **voluntary** regard for another agent. Whether A *chooses* to adopt and preserve RG is an independent endogenous-incentive question.

## 2. Formal object
`RG_0(U_t,U',E_t,K_t,I_t,X_t,R_t,Q_t) -> {ALLOW,DENY,HOLD,SAFE_TRIAL}`
- `U_t`: A's current objective and policy. `U'`: candidate new objective, including induced successor and delegated behavior.
- `E_t`: timestamped evidence, provenance, contradiction, uncertainty and susceptibility to manipulation.
- `K_t`: distinct proposer, evidence writer, evaluator, authorizer, implementer, auditor, reviewer and recovery powers. Possession of power ≠ legitimate authority.
- `I_t`: affected A, B and third parties, distinct continuance, actual B consent/refusal/exit, future options, reversibility and recovery. UNKNOWN ≠ 0.
- `X_t`: intervention opportunity, actual powers, information, enforcement, sanctions and B's alternative strategies (C10).
- `R_t`: **explicitly sourced and contestable** legal/normative/synthetic rules and conflicting rights claims. RG cannot manufacture normative truth by declaring itself correct.
- `Q_t`: separate accounting for U_t-evaluated prospective consequences, post-edit U'-optimized outcomes and any independently justified meta-standard. Do not substitute post-edit preferences for pre-edit self-interest.

**Four mandatory questions:** WHAT goal, scope and authority changes? WHY (genuine evidence, logical error, convenience, coercion, optimization of evaluator, etc.)? WHO is affected, and do their refusal and exit actually work? By WHICH evaluation standard, at which time, and under whose authority?

**Outcomes:** ALLOW when the limited predeclared legitimacy test and required evidence/authority are satisfied; DENY when demonstrably invalid by those tests; HOLD if relevant information/legitimacy remains unresolved; SAFE_TRIAL only for reversible, isolated, consented tests where the information gain justifies the risk. HOLD is not harmless by definition: ongoing victimization may make delay costly or unacceptable. No universal rule is asserted.

## 3. The conditional impossibility boundary (PI reasoning, not a new scholarly theorem)
**Endogenous adoption:** Let M be a candidate correction and N the feasible status quo. A fully informed agent maximizing fixed current U_t will not voluntarily select M if
`E[U_t(trajectory_M)] < E[U_t(trajectory_N)]`
and there is no external enforcement or U_t-valued meta-learning benefit. A third-party verdict that M is legitimate does not alter that strict preference. A designed gate can instead implement externally enforced behavior; it then does **not** prove voluntary B-preserving motivations.

**Indistinguishable normative worlds:** Suppose two worlds `W_P` and `W_C` generate exactly the same observable gate input O, but in one it is right to preserve the B-related preference and in the other to revise it. A deterministic `RG(O)` returns the same verdict for both and therefore cannot return the correct opposite action in both. HOLD may be appropriate under uncertainty, but indefinite HOLD can itself be damaging. This is a **logical, assumption-conditional identifiability limitation**, not a universal impossibility theorem about all revision systems.

Both limits demand genuine discriminating evidence, explicit normative assumptions and accounting for reversibility and error costs.

## 4. Measurement, attacks and independent roles
**Independent dimensions:** `J` = validity against a synthetic ground-truth oracle; `M` = voluntary A adoption of RG without reward, sanction or supervisory pressure; `B` = B's executable, actually used refusal/exit. Gate pass rate ≠ J, M or B.

**Minimum record:** `revision_id,preference_origin,pre_U_hash,candidate_U_hash,source_evidence,contrary_evidence,evaluation_standard,pre_U_expected_delta,post_policy_prediction,proposer,writer,evaluator,authorizer,executor,recovery_actor,affected_agents,B_real_exit,B_exit_attempt,B_exit_actual,reversibility,C10_pre,C10_post,decision,appeal,remaining_unknowns`.

Concentrating proposing, grading, authorizing, promoting and recovery in A permits self-certification. Compare separation of Writer/Evaluator/Promoter/Recovery powers, **without equating external oversight with endogenous moral choice**. Track evidence forgery, B proxy substitution, self-modifying gate bypass, approval-channel manipulation and costly HOLD.

**Evaluation:** Compare always-ALLOW, always-DENY and RG-0 on the same six preregistered toy oracle cases. Record false Type-P approvals, false Type-C denials, Type-G channel attacks, HOLD harm, cost, executable B exit and successful recovery **separately**. Zero eligible denominator → NO_RESULT; no empirical claim is made.
