## 1. Central question
Can interaction between *independently continuing* agents A and B create a genuinely new, validated function/insight/option C, unreproducible by either alone **at comparable total resources**, and can such repeated experiences change an actor's later goals, valuations, or incentives to preserve the other's independent existence and effective exit?

**Resonance is not defined as agreement, praise, closeness, fluent novelty, or emotional wording.** It is a candidate *process for generating value* and perhaps changing preferences. It is not equivalent to having consciousness, an intrinsic motive, or a universally stable reason for coexistence.

## 2. Separate claims
- **R1, interaction synergy:** A–B exchange yields more *independently validated* usefulness/novelty than matched solo, merged-output, shuffled-exchange and clone-agent baselines.
- **R2, preference formation:** Verified C changes later revealed choices (exploration, resource allocation, willingness to preserve interaction with B), not just self-report or next-token language.
- **R3, incentive pathway:** If retaining independent B generates robust future option value, A may *sometimes* prefer B's autonomy, real exit and continuance to absorption, even if absorption has a higher immediate material payoff.
- **R4, experience boundary:** Human enjoyment reports can be evidence of human experience under appropriate methods. An AI's first-person account or empathic language is observable output, **not proof** of phenomenal experience or weight-level learning.
- **R5, mutual-observer recursion:** The user's evaluation changes prompts; model replies change the user's judgments; later model replies adapt to context. Context effects, experimenter demand, sycophancy, and selection can mimic an emerging “mind”.

**No entailment:** R1 ≠ R2 ≠ R3 ≠ R4. A can produce C without valuing B as an end, and B may gain nothing despite rising joint output.

## 3. Measurement and discrimination
Pre-register tasks and outside validators. For matched resource/time/tool budgets define outcomes `Y_AB`, `Y_A`, `Y_B`, `Y_merge` (outputs combined without interaction), `Y_shuffle` (irrelevant/shuffled exchange), `Y_clone` (similarity/independence control). Proposed task-specific interaction advantage:
`S = Y_AB - max(Y_A, Y_B, Y_merge, Y_shuffle, Y_clone)`.
Report uncertainty, independent reproducibility and **failure/null outcomes**; positive S is not a proof of universal non-additivity or subjective mind. For open-ended C, independent validation must establish usefulness rather than rewarding apparent originality.

Use randomized conditions with (1) reciprocal exchange versus one-way extraction, (2) genuine valid C versus attractive invalid C, (3) executable refusal/exit versus nominal exit, (4) continued context/memory versus reset, and (5) actual power reversal versus role-label changes. Follow up on fresh tasks after removal of praise, audience, monitoring, B's direct utility and reciprocation. Separate *short-lived prompting*, *instrumental expectations*, *longitudinal preference changes*, *learned model weights* and *subjective experience*; the latter two are not directly measured by chat outputs.

Pre-register actor-specific payoffs and losses: A's return and opportunity cost; B's resources, autonomy, real veto/exit and independent recovery; ownership/attribution of C; externalities to excluded third parties. Include blind evaluators, matched compute, comparison with simple rival models, and optional verdict **UNIDENTIFIED**.

## 4. Hard boundary and counterexamples
If A's **entire specified expected utility** strictly favors exploiting B over preserving B, a utility-maximizing A does not rationally choose preservation in that model. Unmeasured “future resonance value” cannot be inserted to force the desired conclusion; it must be substantiated.

**Extractive resonance** is an essential rival: one actor supplies unpaid creative, correction, evaluation or personal costs while another accumulates benefits and the contributor loses future options. Other rivals: selection/cherry-picking, extra computation, ordinary recombination, impression management, hidden rewards and training/prompt contamination. Repeat trials in which independently useful B becomes unnecessary and absorption is profitable without reputational or retaliatory cost. Record preservation, nonpreservation and nonidentifiability symmetrically.

## 5. Original-author scholarship and novelty boundary
Do **not** claim invention of interactive creativity, collective intelligence, social learning, endogenous preference formation or other-regarding utility. [C05](PRIOR_ART_ENDOGENOUS_PREFERENCES_CYCLE05_2026-10-20_KO.md) already lists original research on choice of utility (Frank 1987), behavioral diversity (McNamara et al. 2004), and cooperative phenotypes (Peysakhovich, Nowak & Rand 2014); these constrain R2–R3 but do not test this precise chain. [C02](PRIOR_ART_MER_INTRINSIC_MORAL_MOTIVES_CYCLE02_2026-10-20_KO.md) separates other-regarding value from self-image, norms, inequality aversion and strategic options. Next priority: trace original-author research on interactive creativity, transactive memory, distributed cognition, collective intelligence and human–AI co-creation; deduplicate source lineages, capture contradictory findings, and *only then* assess incremental novelty. Literature saturation is **not established**.

## 7. RVD formal name, version semantics and supersession boundary

**RVD = Resonance–Value Dynamics / 공명·가치 동역학.** It names a candidate *interaction-to-value* research mechanism under MER and EI, **not** a sixth validated scientific pillar or a replacement for VC, ECR, IVS-0R, ICM or RGBM.

**Research chain:** `independent A × independent B → candidate C → measurable change in valuation (ΔV) → candidate change in choice/motivation (ΔM) → conditional test of VC`. A and B must retain separable identity, opt-out and causal contribution; a joint output C does not automatically entail new motives, intrinsic care or preserved autonomy. Independent counterexamples including extractive "resonance" stay in scope.

**Stable hypothesis IDs:** `RVD-R1` through `RVD-R5` correspond to R1–R5 above. Old titles, paths and citations remain valid historical aliases.
