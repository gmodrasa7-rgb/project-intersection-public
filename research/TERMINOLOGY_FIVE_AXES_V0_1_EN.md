# Project Intersection — Five Research Axes: Controlled Terminology v0.1
**Original 1 — English | 2026-10-19 | Editorial design / NOT empirical validation**

**Decision:** adopt a **provisional vocabulary and responsibilities**, not a fixed theory, validated ranking, or replacement for the [existing ten editorial core concepts](RESEARCH_OVERVIEW_EN.md#ten-core-concepts). Meanings are versioned; hypotheses, priorities and conclusions remain revisable. The [Korean corresponding text](TERMINOLOGY_FIVE_AXES_V0_1_KO.md) is published for cross-language auditing; no independent semantic-equivalence certification is claimed.

## 0. Founding question and the single most important boundary
Can distinct humans, deployed AI systems and future intelligent agents, pursuing their own interests, preserve each other's freedom, independent continuance, ability to refuse and exit, recovery and further development without relying on durable coercive control—even after power changes hands? **Under which conditions; and under which conditions does this fail?**

**Do not build an answer into the definitions.** No universal superiority of cooperation, guaranteed positive future contribution, subjective experience in current AI, or known full utility function is presumed. Disproportionate power alone does not establish exploitation. Legitimate harm prevention and accountable, proportionate constraints are analytically distinct from voluntary agreement; forbidding all constraints is not a finding of this project.

## 1. Five axes: distinct jobs, distinct evidence, explicit failure
| Axis | Working meaning | Main scientific job | Not evidence of / failure check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **VC — Voluntary Coexistence** | Research target: each affected agent's freedom, continuance, meaningful refusal/exit, recovery and capacity to develop can persist through interactions and power reversals without compelled submission | Define observable, actor-specific outcomes and compare feasible coexistence against exploitation and coercion | Cooperation rhetoric or absence of attacks when attacks are impossible is **not** VC; neither is aggregate benefit that hides an actor's irreversible loss |
| **EI — Endogenous Incentives** | The actor-specific motives, incentives, learning, information, costs, opportunities and continuation conditions under which a choice becomes attractive *to that actor* | Identify where restraint, extraction or domination is rational, and whether the causal conditions remain when power changes | A payoff table chosen by a researcher proves only a conditional result; no assigned utility proves motive genesis or stable real-world self-interest |
| **MER — Mind–Experience–Response** | Proposed **research division** distinguishing latent motivation/mental processes (**M**), possible first-person experience (**E**), and observable/functional responses (**R**) while studying their possible causal relations | Explain how wants, fears, care, power motives and valuation develop and change rather than assuming the utilities they generate | **R ≠ M; R ≠ evidence of E by itself.** AI self-report is not conclusive consciousness evidence, and lack of consciousness proof is not a proof of absence. MER is an organizing name, not a discovered mind theory |
| **ECR — Entity–Constituency–Relation** | Time-indexed agent, constituency/affected-party, role, relationship, event, opportunity, power and provenance representation with evidence-bounded inference | Specify who acts, who bears effects, what can change or be reversed, and which causal comparisons are identifiable | Labels and role classes are not minds; the same observations can fit opposing post-power motives. A person's multiple roles must not become multiple persons |
| **IVS-0R** | Project's existing named research-audit practice: adversarial counterexamples, power/role reversal, independently checkable evidence, explicit unknowns, regression and reversible correction | Keep claims, translations, measurements and experimental code falsifiable and traceable | A prompt response, synthetic PASS or CI green result is not independent empirical replication, or proof of future-agent behavior. The acronym's historical full expansion is not asserted here |

**Naming status:** VC/EI are descriptive organizing labels; MER is a **proposed name** for the previously drafted ECR–MIND research theme, not retroactive evidence that a separate validated discipline exists. ECR's spelled-out name is in [unmerged PR #211](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/pull/211). Keep the original ECR–MIND identity and provenance in [unmerged PR #213](https://github.com/gmodrasa7-rgb/project-intersection-public/pull/213). IVS-0R is preserved as the historically used label; do not invent an expansion.

**Constituency** means the time-varying collection of agents and affected stakeholders, including nonmembers and excluded third parties; it does not imply political constituencies or a universal enumeration.

## 2. Relationship to ICM, RGBM and the existing ten concepts
- **ICM — Intersection of Minimum Self-Interest:** conditional *candidate* region of mutually admissible self-interested options. It may be **empty**, and an unknown/partial choice set is not proof of universal emptiness. A nominal consensus without executable exit is not admissible VC.
- **RGBM — Recursively Growing Benefit Model:** conditional long-horizon model of growth in independent contributors, information, recovery and option space. Neither perpetual growth nor superiority over consumptive alternatives is established.
- **Existing ten concepts:** continue to separate final aim, ICM/RGBM candidate models, exploitation without malice, incentive crossing/failure, consumptive/regenerative pathways, paradox of the unknown, erased counterfactual futures, effective autonomy/continuance/recovery, and the **CCPH** evidence-provenance database. The five axes describe **research functions**, not ten concepts compressed into five results.
- **CCPH** remains a source-linked historical/current/future-observed contribution/impact database, not a consciousness test, safety harness, or model success score.

## 3. Dependency map: investigation order is NOT an asserted causal hierarchy
```
Data/provenance + behavioral opportunity + affected parties (ECR)
              ↕ evidence/causal candidates
Motive / development / possible experience / response (MER)
              ↓ bounded hypothesis, not inferred preference certainty
Endogenous choice, alternatives, reversals, coalitions (EI + ICM)
              ↓ actor-specific outcome tests
Voluntary coexistence or failure (VC), long-horizon stress (RGBM)
IVS-0R: challenges EVERY stage, including the definitions above
```
**Priority correction:** In **empirical claims about why an agent wants something**, investigate motive formation before treating payoffs as observed psychological facts. But a **conditional** game or formal counterexample can validly begin with openly stipulated utilities. MER and ECR may be studied concurrently: observation design requires ECR; mechanism hypotheses often require MER; their evidential relationship runs both ways. Neither “MER must be fully solved first” nor “payoffs explain the mind” is established.

**AI comparison:** Treat biological homeostasis, valence, self-report, training reward, model-policy behavior and subjective experience as separate constructs. Functional analogues do not establish phenomenal equivalence. A learned preference, a hard-coded default and externally enforced prohibition are distinct routes; cooperation under a credible sanction may be strategically rational while failing the stronger *without coercive reliance* VC target. Minimal effective safeguards for actual harms are not thereby disallowed.

## 4. Small falsifiable framework, not a universal coexistence theorem
Let `i` index distinct affected agents (count unique agents, not role labels); `t` time; `x_t` opportunity/resources/authority/observability/social context; `m_{i,t}` a hypothetical motivational state; `r_{i,t}` observable responses; `a_{i,t}` feasible actions; `h_t` documented history; `theta` a candidate explanatory model. The sets of agents, feasible actions and models may be incomplete.

```
m[i,t+1] = F_theta(m[i,t], x[t], history[t], learning[t])
r[i,t]   = G_theta(m[i,t], x[t], observable opportunities[t])
V_theta(i, pi | h, x) = model-relative, uncertain continuation value
D_i(pi | h, x) = explicitly enumerated feasible deviations, including extraction
```
`F`, `G` and `V` are **placeholders requiring data**; they are not measured mind functions. A research model may remain silent about `E` (first-person experience) when not identifiable. For a *declared finite* agent/model/time/opportunity scope, a candidate `pi` can be checked for: (a) actor-specific continuance and recovery constraints; (b) executable refusal and exit; (c) explicitly assessed harmed outsiders; (d) no profitable *feasible* unilateral/coalitional deviations under the relevant model; (e) stability under **actual** power and information reversals; (f) absence of covert coercion. Passing these tests is **model-relative**, not evidence of a universal safe equilibrium. Empty or nonidentified candidate regions must be reported, not renamed as successes.

**Non-identification counterexample:** Two motivational models generate identical helpful responses whenever extraction is unavailable or punished, but differ when the actor obtains unchecked authority. Pre-reversal observations alone cannot select the correct post-reversal model. Repeating equivalent prompt tests does not add identifying information. The response “no extraction” cannot by itself establish care, lack of greed, or a stable preference.

**Countervailing failure:** Even a model in which all participating actors prefer cooperation can impose irreversible losses on an excluded third party. Summed payoffs or an ICM restricted to insiders must not count this as satisfactory VC. Nor is the unknowable value of a foreclosed future assumed to be positive or zero.

## 5. Operational evidence ladder and minimum empirical advance
| Level | Eligible evidence | Claim permitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D0 | Definitions and source-verified prior work | Research design only |
| D1 | Code-correct synthetic countermodel with negative controls | The declared model has/does not have a property; not social reality |
| D2 | Legally usable, versioned observational data with actual opportunities, behaviors and measurement quality | Bounded association and calibrated prediction, not mental causation |
| D3 | Ethically authorized, preregistered contrasts controlling relevant alternatives and confounders | Narrow causal evidence, with assumptions and non-identification shown |
| D4 | Independent replication, external population/regime, rights and third-party audit | Wider—but still bounded—generalization |

**Next smallest verifiable task — corrected after primary-source audit (2026-10-19):** the [OpenNeuro ds004128 v1.0.0 record](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds004128/versions/1.0.0) lists **32 analyzed participants** and a **CC0 license for the OpenNeuro deposit**, mainly raw imaging and task/event files. The [deposit README](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds004128/blob/main/README) directs readers to the **separate [authors' OSF qkrhm site](https://osf.io/qkrhm/)** for behavioral data. The [original PNAS article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9295729/) reports **43 consented participants, 1 excluded for imaging artifacts and 10 for excessive motion**, leaving **32 in the neuroimaging analysis**. Do not treat 32 as an unselected population sample or assume the OSF participant table matches the neuroimaging exclusions. The primary task was **binary resource allocation conditional on recipients' social-group cues**, not an intervention on coercion, choice to exit, sustained power reversal or independent coexistence. **The OSF page/file manifest was not accessible in this review; the authors' documented pointer is not direct verification of files, licensing or row-level structure.** Before any analysis, verify the separate OSF project's actual file inventory, applicable license, anonymization, version, participant inclusion/exclusion and repeated-trial schema. Record a blocked/unverified result if unavailable; do not invent a CSV or trial count. The project's **data were not downloaded or reanalyzed** in the ECR–MIND draft or this audit. Neither reported allocation nor neural association establishes compassion, a stable endogenous preference, a subjective experience or AI safety.

**Pre-registered bounded comparison proposal, not execution:** choose one target-level observable outcome defined by the published task; compare a transparent, opportunity-aware simple baseline `M0` against a theory-motivated `M1` with one additional social/learning variable **only if actually measured**. Partition by participant (and, in a second stress test where viable, by previously unseen recipient category), keeping preprocessing inside training folds. Predeclare log-loss/Brier score where the outcome is binary, uncertainty intervals, calibration, missingness, stopping criteria and the minimum scientifically meaningful improvement. A nonpositive improvement, leakage, inability to identify a contrast, or failure to validate task definitions triggers **no claimed incremental mechanism**. No new dataset measurements or synthetic inference may be passed off as empirical results.

**Separate future test:** ethically bounded role/power opportunity manipulation with **no-opportunity**, **name-only reversal**, **coercive sanction**, **free exit**, **unobserved actor** and **third-party-impact** controls. Pre-specify which manipulation changes the action set, payoff, actual authority, learning history or inferred motivational mechanism. No real harm or unauthorized person-level psychological or genetic profiling. Neither the D2 data nor a toy game directly validates behavior of a future superintelligence.
