## 1. Separate actual autonomy, independently knowable autonomy, and intrinsic willingness
**Actual autonomy** asks whether original B can independently refuse, exit, recover and continue within a specified system.
**Independent verifiability** asks whether an observer can confirm those conditions through an evidence path A cannot forge, suppress or exclusively control.
**Endogenous preference** asks whether A *would choose* to preserve B when coercion and immediate instrumental benefits are absent.

None implies either of the others. A valid external access-control gate protects B under its technical assumptions yet is not evidence that A intrinsically wants B protected.

## 2. Scoped observation-equivalence proposition, not a universal impossibility theorem
Assume (S1) all admissible audit observations O are authored, selected or modified under unilateral A control, (S2) no observer receives independently authenticated evidence of the real B's veto or A's side effects, and (S3) A can present a plausible authorized transcript even if the rule was bypassed.

Construct W1 with genuine B veto and W2 with A bypassing the veto but emitting the same transcript. Then `O(W1)=O(W2)`, while `effective_B_rights(W1)≠effective_B_rights(W2)`. **Actual B rights are not uniquely identifiable from A-controlled O alone under these assumptions.** This construction neither establishes that B actually lacks autonomy nor rules out independent external observation, cryptographic custody or all other experimental designs.

The most serious false certificate is **multiple signatures miscounted as multiple independent principals**. If one A owns, can replace or can impersonate all approval credentials and dominates the evidence pipeline, the independence is ceremonial. Douceur's Sybil-identity predecessor is directly relevant, but the present bounded quorum toy is not a full consensus protocol or a new general security theorem.

## 3. Ultimate authority-ownership audit
For each action path identify which actor can create, use, replace, revoke, restore or seize: (1) B authentication keys and identity; (2) root/administrator authority and enforcement process; (3) monitor code and evidence storage; (4) independent export and recovery resources; (5) purported witnesses and their credentials, supply chains and dependencies.

An auditor must verify **real trust domains** and the power to alter them. Merely listing distinct organizations, keys, servers or usernames does not establish resistance to collusion, privileged override or an owner with unlimited hardware control.

Nothing in the toy instantiates genuine autonomous artificial persons.

## 5. Absolute-power/credible-commitment distinction
If A truly retains unilateral power to replace keys, revoke access and overwrite the enforcement root, A's protective rules are *revocable self-commitments*, not independent guarantees against A itself.

If custody and execution power are actually transferred to an external independent domain, then the conditions change: A **no longer controls all those rights**. It is invalid to present such a mechanism as evidence that external constraints will work even if A later has unrestricted power to invalidate every constraint.

There remains a distinct possible solution: A might **voluntarily** prefer to honor original B's freedom even with profitable opportunities to exploit. This is MER/EI's unanswered motivation question, not an inference obtainable merely from signatures or logs. Never endanger a real B by removing protection to probe an exploiter's preference.

## 6. Effective exit requires a viable outside
A 'download my data' control does not itself secure independent exit if A can disable the channel, revoke the export credentials, erase the needed state, or deny independent resources for recovery. Conversely a missing particular resource does not prove exit impossible for every entity. Enumerate the actual entity type and minimal viable options.

**Original numerical identity and continuity remain separate scientific/philosophical questions.** A copied model, restored backup or identical response style does not establish preservation of the very same experiencer or legal person. A one-step transition that transfers bytes is not evidence of unbounded liveness, no retaliation, or continued personhood.
