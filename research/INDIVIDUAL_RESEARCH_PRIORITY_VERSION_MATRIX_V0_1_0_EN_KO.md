## Original 1 — English

**Purpose:** rank the **same 24 individual research topics** as the project taxonomy, including nested research submodules, without pretending these are 24 independently validated scientific discoveries. Version labels are preserved **as originally attested**; this matrix adds separate planning metadata.

**Version display contract:** `vX.Y.Z | P0–P3 | N1–N5/5 | D1–D5/5`. The existing component's semantic version is **unchanged**; P (priority), N (necessity), and D (difficulty) are attributes of **this matrix v0.1.0**, not digits in a scientific maturity score.

**N (necessity):** 5 essential to determining the central coexistence proposition; 4 important dependency; 3 conditional support; 2 distant application; 1 peripheral. **D (difficulty):** 5 very difficult latent motive/causal or long-run identification; 4 challenging interdisciplinary mechanism/data; 3 bounded but nontrivial cross-source work; 2 established methods; 1 limited scoped review. Both are **subjective editorial planning judgements**, not measured probabilities, costs, expert consensus, fixed actor features or AI capability scores.

**P (execution priority):** P0 core foundational 1–5; P1 high-impact next-stage 6–13; P2 supporting mechanisms 14–20; P3 downstream or specialized 21–24. Rank is a transparent work queue based on conceptual dependence, feasible falsification, and relevance to individual autonomy **not a mathematical optimization of N/D**. Difficult crucial research remains high priority. First operational gate for every topic is prior-art collection → original source deduplication → negative/rival evidence → explicit missing-data map, before new experiments.

**평가 대상:** 기존에 분류한 **개별 연구 24개**. PACD 하위 연구는 작업 단위로 표시하지만 서로 독립된 과학적 발견 24건이라고 주장하지 않음.

**버전 표기 규칙:** `vX.Y.Z | P0~P3 | N1~N5/5 | D1~D5/5`. 연구 자체 버전은 그대로 두고, **이 우선순위 행렬 v0.1.0**이 계획상 지표를 붙인다. 연구 버전 상승과 실증성·성공률 상승을 동일시하지 않는다.
