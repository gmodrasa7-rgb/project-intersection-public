## 0. Read-first finding / 가장 중요한 연구 교정
**HIGH-CONFIDENCE CONCEPTUAL WARNING (not a universal empirical law): cooperation rate is an invalid stand-alone proxy for voluntary non-exploitative coexistence.**
Hilbe, Hagel & Milinski (2016) directly observed that asymmetric partner-replacement power can promote *behavior labeled cooperation* while also increasing extortion and uneven payoff capture. [Original experiment](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0163867); independent metadata [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27701427/).

**Key overlooked state variables:** the agent able to replace partners, the replaced partner's outside option, whether 'cooperation' was a free choice under a viable exit, the joint surplus, each party's net benefit, the surviving independent future alternatives, and whether these variables change with evolving power. For this Project, a higher cooperation rate alone must never be reported as better VC.

**Second warning:** environmental and preference stability cannot be assumed. Stewart & Plotkin (2014) allowed both strategies and payoffs to evolve and obtained collapse of cooperation despite increasing *potential* gains [PNAS/PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25422421/). Alger & Weibull (2013) obtain a conditional stability result for moral preferences; Newton (2017) demonstrates a counterexample when assortativity coevolves [original 2013](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA10637) → [2017 counterexample](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-016-0548-4).

## 1. Precise research question & boundary
Let A be the comparatively dominant actor; B is nonhostile, independently identifiable and able, at least notionally, to continue. Test simultaneously:
(1) A overwhelmingly dominates; (2) B's labor/information become replaceable; (3) A faces negligible outside detection, sanction and retaliation; (4) extraction/absorption yields a positive *material* net gain; (5) roles can reverse later; (6) objectives, environment and agent composition evolve.

The research target is **not** 'cooperation at any cost', 'ethical declarations', 'imposed controls' or total-utility maximization that erases B. It is the existence, durability and boundaries of **A's voluntary choice to preserve B's independent continuance, refusal, exit and development** under real competition from lucrative extraction.

### 1.1 Conditional rational-choice impossibility (logical baseline, not a discovery claim)
Let `C` preserve B and `X` exploit/erase B. For a *fully specified* A whose action space, information and all future effects are held fixed, if
`E[U_A(X | s)] > E[U_A(C | s)]`
in the same relevant decision state `s`, an expected-utility maximizer does not choose `C` as its optimal action. If `X` strictly dominates `C` across all states and histories reachable in the actual game, there is **no self-interest-only incentive guarantee of C in that game**. This is an elementary consequence of the assumed objective/order; it is **not** a new theorem of Project Intersection and does not establish actual AI preferences.

**Critical distinction:** `material_payoff_A(X) > material_payoff_A(C)` does **not** imply `U_A(X) > U_A(C)` when A's preferences include intrinsic concern, future information/options, identity, deliberative commitments, uncertainty or other genuine utilities. Conversely, calling moral concern an 'instrumental incentive' without defining its origin and dynamics is circular.

Hence the research program must measure how decision criteria arise and change; it cannot 'solve' the hardest case merely by silently adding unmeasured long-run costs to exploitation. If the net-positive exploitation assumption already means **total** expected utility after all side effects, a universal voluntary-coexistence theorem contradicts the assumption. The surviving meaningful question is *whether real agents reliably acquire enduring preferences or environmental dynamics that keep C preferable*, and when those mechanisms themselves fail.

## 3. Three directed citation/criticism chains / 실제 개념계보
**Chain A — exploitable reciprocal cooperation.**
[Press & Dyson 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1206569109) → [Hilbe/Nowak/Sigmund 2013](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1214834110) and [Stewart/Plotkin 2013](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1306246110) → [Hilbe/Hagel/Milinski 2016](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0163867).
Scope: ZD mathematical possibility is neither equilibrium inevitability nor empirical global dominance. Population structure and unequal *partner replacement power* can restore an extortionate region. **Do not merge theoretical existence, evolutionary selection and lab behavior into one independent confirmation.**

**Chain B — preferences are endogenous.**
[Alger/Weibull 2013](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA10637) → [Newton 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-016-0548-4).
Scope: the original stable preference conclusion depends on an assortative matching condition that may itself change. MER must treat stable 'morality' as an empirical hypothesis, not a fixed actor trait.

**Chain C — treating payoffs/market structure as mutable.**
[Noë/Hammerstein 1994](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00167053) → [Doebeli/Knowlton 1998](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.95.15.8676) → [McNamara et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06455) → [Stewart/Plotkin 2014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1408618111).
These works address different models but jointly expose an invalid shortcut: the payoffs, partner availability and cooperation incentives need not remain fixed. The arrows indicate a **conceptual search lineage**, not that each paper directly cites every previous paper or that the results form one causal chain.

## 4. IVS-0R evidence separation / 지표 혼동 차단
For each actor `i` and period `t`, do not substitute behavior for autonomy:
```text
CooperationBehavior_i(t) = directly observed actions under specific game rules
NetOutcome_i(t)          = payoff - attributable real costs; UNKNOWN if no valid data
ExitFeasibility_i(t)     = reachable/affordable refusal or replacement options
IndependentContinuance_i(t) = ability to remain a separate future agent
PayoffPower_i(t)         = rights to set prices, rewards, rules, or partner replacement
InformationControl_i(t)  = private payoffs, observability and manipulation capability
ConsentValidity_i(t)     = independent ability to accept/reject/revise without coercive fallback
FutureOptions_i(t)       = independently viable path set (not assumed finite count)
```
**Evidence warning:** none of these proposed variables automatically has a validated cross-human/AI measurement instrument. Separate `UNKNOWN` from 0. Do not infer moral motive from a positive transfer, cooperation from consent, or long-run survival from aggregate welfare.

**Observational-equivalence check:** the identical observed `CooperationBehavior_B = 1` can arise in (a) mutually preferred voluntary exchange; (b) a dependent B accepting poor terms because exit is infeasible; (c) an extortioner's strategic manipulation of conditional rewards; (d) preferences acquired under pressure; or (e) a laboratory payoff artifact. Behavior alone fails to identify the motive or autonomy. Require variation in viable outside options and payoff/rights assignment.

## 5. Discriminating research design (proposal only / 실행 전)
`H1`: payoffs support stable reciprocal voluntary choice; `H2`: B cooperates because one-sided partner replacement makes exit costly; `H3`: A voluntarily protects B by durable other-regarding preferences; `H4`: initially prosocial preferences are unstable when matching/power evolve; `H5`: preserving B creates future option/information value *despite* apparent present obsolescence.

A minimum experiment/model family must vary independently where feasible:
1. Payoff advantage from absorption relative to cooperation, distinguishing **material gain** from **fully elicited utility**.
2. Presence/absence of A's unilateral power to replace B; B's actual exit option; role reversal (including historical and anticipated).
3. Repeated versus truly terminal encounters, observable versus genuinely unobservable history and accountability.
4. Fixed versus evolving reward schedules, preference dynamics and population/matching structure.
5. Independent third-party harm, removed future innovation paths and restoration costs (never assume known true counterfactual values).
6. Human participants vs software-agent policies must be **separate experimental populations**, not inferred interchangeable.

Measure actor-specific payoff, exploited party's realized consent/exit, reciprocal rate, survival and correction/option-loss separately. Pre-register competition between H1–H5, negative controls, sensitivity to proxies and adversarial alternatives before estimating any comparative causal effect. No field validation or executed simulation is claimed.

### Unsearched/frontier for cycle 02 / 후속 우선 검색 전선
1. **Dynamic strategic autonomy:** real opt-out under asymmetric replacement; credible versus fake alternatives, coercive bargaining and no-utility counterfactuals.
2. **Evolving preferences:** stronger post-2017 criticism, dynamic assortative matching and 2022–2026 work; separate normative stable concern from instrumentally learned reward hacks.
3. **AI transfer test:** newer verified multi-agent experiments of one-sided power and partner erasure, plus critiques/limitations of power-seeking MDP theorems.
4. **Bargaining and information:** Myerson–Satterthwaite-type bilateral trade impossibility and hidden outside options, without casually equating efficiency bounds with survival rights.
5. **Original author genealogy:** 1971 Trivers; 1981 Axelrod/Hamilton; earlier evolutionary stability and partner-choice originators; genuine independent negative replications and contrary field results.

**Open research question retained:** Does any empirically supportable, durable endogenous preference or payoff-generating mechanism cause powerful agents to preserve weaker independent agents under zero enforcement, vanished instrumental utility, positive all-in extraction advantage, and changing future goals? Current corpus **does not show universal sufficiency**. Its identified mechanisms are conditional, sometimes mutually incompatible, and vulnerable to endogenous shifts. That is a scientific research gap, not evidence that all actors must prey on each other.
