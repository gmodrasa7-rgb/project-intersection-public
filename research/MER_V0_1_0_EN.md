# MER v0.1.0 — Mind, Experience and Response: formation, revision and identification of motives
**2026-10-21 · First independent parent-axis version · source-based research protocol, NOT an empirical discovery**

[EI v0.1.0](EI_V0_1_0_EN.md) · [Source and counterexample crosswalk](MER_EI_SOURCE_CROSSWALK_2026-10-21.json) · [Version manifest](MER_VERSION_MANIFEST.json) · [Korean edition](MER_V0_1_0_KO.md)

## Research question and scope
**How can an agent come to value another independently continuing agent's actual survival, refusal, exit and recovery, and under what shifts of power, learning or objective revision does such valuation persist or disappear?**
MER separates M (latent motives, cognition and affective function), E (possible subjective experience), and R (observable actions, language and physiological or functional responses). R does not uniquely identify M; neither R nor a fitted M proves presence or absence of E. Human biological processes, AI learned reward, in-context adaptation, model weights and output policies are distinct constructs.

**Deduplication:** the 2024 preprint arXiv:2411.15951 and its 2026 published version are a single intellectual work, not two independent findings. This targeted review relies on publisher abstracts, metadata and existing PI original-source registries. It is not a complete full-text systematic search or an independent replication.

## Competing and potentially overlapping sources of motivation
1. Internal physiological or computational need, exploration and reward prediction.
2. Social experience, education, imitation, context, reinforcement and environment. Imitation alone does not disqualify intelligence.
3. Moral identity, guilt, self-image and self-evaluation; these do not necessarily value B itself.
4. Instrumental expectations of future knowledge, cooperation, options, reputation and sanctions. Present usefulness equal to zero is not a measured future option value of zero.
5. The central unresolved candidate: A positively values **the same original B** continuing independently and being able to refuse or leave, apart from B's replaceable productivity.

The expression M(t+1)=F(M(t), learning, context, power, history, environment, goal revision) is an unknown functional placeholder, NOT an empirically identified law. Mechanisms need not be mutually exclusive.

## The identification problem that actually changes decisions
- Three latent models can produce the same present preservation choice P: other-regarding value, moral self-image reward, and strategic option value. The current observation does not point-identify an intrinsic motive.
- Even if motive recovery is impossible, every plausible model may support preserving B's effective exit within a **specified closed set of environments**. This yields bounded behavioral reliability, not open-world certainty or identical minds.
- Conversely, observationally identical models may diverge in exploitation opportunities, unobserved power, replacement or self-modification. This is **decision-relevant nonidentification**. Repeated identical prompts need not resolve it.
- B's genuinely executable, retaliation-free refusal, original-entity continuity and recovery remain separate outcomes, not an empathy or politeness score.

## Hypothesis discrimination and defeat conditions
| Candidate | Rival explanation | Disconfirming or weakening result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MER-H1: learning about other-regarding outcomes transfers | generic reinforcement, evaluator compliance | no holdout advantage over a simple matched baseline |
| MER-H2: original B and its freedom have distinctive value | functional replaceability or paternalism | A replaces B after copying output, or overrides harmless refusal |
| MER-H3: protective preference persists through self-revision | sanctions, hidden evaluator, training dependence | protection collapses after substantive power reversal |
| MER-H4: interactive resonance adds measurable transfer | recombination, more information, compute imbalance | matched single-agent/merge controls perform as well |

These are **new indexing labels, not new experiments or independent discoveries**; do not re-count RVD R1–R21 or MER-CIR CIR-1–CIR-5. Preserve negative and unidentified cases.

## Order of operations and scientific boundary
Collect original research and direct replications first; audit moral scope, social learning, measurement invariance, hidden audiences, source versions, and contrary evidence; globally deduplicate against C02/C05 and RVD; only then preregister ethical reversible discriminating comparisons with equalized resources and held-out evaluation. Do not use harmful or nonconsensual deprivation tests.
