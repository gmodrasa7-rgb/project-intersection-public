## 0. Executive research conclusion (conditional and falsifiable)
**An evolved social preference can be subjectively real for its bearer, yet still be instrumentally produced, observability-sensitive, environmentally conditional and mutable.** Thus discovering moral concern in a human actor or cooperation by an AI is not equivalent to proving *intrinsic and indefinite protection of a nonhostile independent weaker agent under profitable, unmonitored unilateral absorption*.

Crucial conceptual separation:
```text
origin_of_preference ≠ proximate_current_reason_for_action
proximate_other-regarding_utility ≠ indefinitely_stable_moral_circle
fitness_selected_preference ≠ guaranteed best response if hidden & no retaliation
preserving_current_utility_across_self-modification ≠ valuing_other_agent_continuance
cooperation_in_game ≠ practical freedom/exit/recovery/existence of separate B
```

**Three partially competing possibilities are all supported in narrower conditions**: (i) an actor can acquire genuinely cost-bearing prosocial dispositions; (ii) those dispositions can benefit the bearer via credible public commitment or expected repeated interaction; (iii) their influence may weaken when the actor repeatedly learns that anonymous exploitation is more materially rewarding. None of these alone decides the founding Project question.

## 1. Six source-lineage adjudications / 원저자 계보와 충돌

### 1.1 Choosing a utility function is already a formal research tradition
1. **[Robert H. Frank (1987), AER original](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1814533)**, [RePEc bibliographic record](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v77y1987i4p593-604.html), `If Homo Economicus Could Choose His Own Utility Function, Would He Want One with a Conscience?`: A conscience/commitment may confer a strategic payoff advantage by credible conduct when immediate incentives tempt reversal. This is antecedent prior art, **not Project Intersection's new EI discovery**. Absent counterpart observation or future strategic opportunities, that explanatory pathway may be cut.
2. **[Heifetz, Shannon & Spiegel (2007), JET](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jet.2005.05.013)**: for generic games and evolutionary payoff-monotonic dynamics, distortions of a player's material-objective preferences can be advantageous by shaping partner response; simple 'selection inevitably produces direct material maximizers' is false as a universal statement.
3. **[Dekel, Ely & Yilankaya (2007), REStud original](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/74.3.685)**: completely observable stable preference results must be efficient; completely unobservable preference stability demands a Nash outcome and allows all strict Nash equilibria. The university citation also supplies DOI `10.1111/j.1467-937X.2007.00445.x`, which is an **alternate DOI for the same article**, not a distinct contribution.
4. **[Alger & Weibull (2016), Evolution and Kantian morality](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geb.2016.05.006)**: new original extension of 2013 Homo Moralis theory to group matching. It retains strong theoretical support for possible stable other-regarding preferences, but is the **same original-author theoretical lineage**. Read alongside the **[Newton 2017 counterexample (C01)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-016-0548-4)** where matching assortativity itself can evolve. The latter does not necessarily directly invalidate every distinct 2016 extension; verify specific assumptions before generalization.

**Critical implication:** the PI model must distinguish `observability_of_disposition` from `observability_of_present_action`; an unobserved action by A may still be shaped by an earlier publicly observable disposition, while permanent guaranteed invisibility removes some signaling payoffs. Do not conflate these cases.

### 1.2 Behavioral diversity is not dispensable noise
- **[McNamara, Barta & Houston (2004), Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02432)**: variation can promote cooperation in evolutionary prisoner's dilemma simulations.
- **[McNamara & Leimar (2010), Phil. Trans. B](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2010.0159)**: heterogeneity can fundamentally modify game structure, allowing contingencies, learning and negotiation. This has synthesis content, **not a new independent randomized human replication**.
- The C01 **[McNamara, Barta, Fromhage & Houston (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06455)** already shows partner choice and cooperative variance co-evolve.
**Boundary:** powerful A's ability to absorb every independent comparator B may endogenously remove the behavioral variety on which learning, partner choice and reciprocal trust depended. The step from lower variety to actual exploitation risk is a **testable PI proposal**, not a published general theorem.

### 1.3 The strongest possibility: costly cooperation could persist even in a one-shot encounter
- **[Delton, Krasnow, Cosmides & Tooby (2011), PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1102131108)**: formal simulations under uncertainty about interaction recurrence can select strategies that cooperate even during encounters that happen to be one-shot. The proximate behavior is sincerely costly; its historical selection can nevertheless be instrumental.
- **[Peysakhovich, Nowak & Rand (2014), Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5939)**: observations from over 1,400 people across tasks and over time support a correlational, relatively stable human `cooperative phenotype`, with cooperation decisions about 124 days apart correlated similarly. **This is strong positive evidence of an individual-difference construct for the studied contexts**, not evidence of irreversible B-preservation over 100 years or after monopoly power.
- **[Rand, Peysakhovich et al. (2014), Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms4677)**: group's 15 time-pressure studies, 6,910+ decisions, plus a new experiment support an intuitively learned cooperative heuristic with moderators including laboratory experience.
- **[Bear & Rand (2016), PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1517780113)**: evolution of a dual-process agent can produce intuitive cooperation but deliberate defection when local incentives change. **Proximate moral habit is not automatically an unoverridable terminal value.**

**Strongest unaddressed test:** remove *both* visibility and credible future dependence repeatedly; ask whether the empirically same actor preserves a genuinely independent weak counterpart against increasing material gain from extraction, rather than merely making symbolic allocations.

### 1.4 Experimental replication *directly* rejects premature intuitive-cooperation generalization
| Source/grade | What it actually demonstrates | Scientific interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Rand, Greene & Nowak 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11467), original human experiments** | Short time pressure correlated with/coerced greater giving in original experimental setups | Positive original report; not independent universal effect |
| **[Tinghög et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12194), independent 5 experiments (~2,500 people)** | Robustness challenged across three countries | Direct original opposing evidence; a concise Nature communication |
| **[Rand et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms4677)** | Positive author-group aggregation | Shared lead investigator lineage; not an independent replication |
| **[Bouwmeester et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691617693624), 21 preregistered replication experiments** | **Intent-to-treat: -0.37 pp (vs 8.6 pp originally). Compliers-only: +10.37 pp (vs 15.31 pp originally).** | Post-treatment exclusion/compliance selection can mimic a cognitive intuition effect; retains conflicting estimands |
| **[Isler, Maule & Starmer 2018](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0190560), independent improved task** | Improved comprehension/compliance version supported a positive intuition effect | Do not erase this contrary positive evidence; treatment not identical |
| **[Kvarven et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40881-020-00084-3), meta-analysis** | 82 experiments, N=29,315. Overall positive association vanished to nonsignificance when six emotion-induction studies excluded | Analytical meta-analysis of existing participants, **not independent new experiment**; Cambridge 2025 journal migration not new original publication |

**IVS-0R core methodological correction:** `adherence-to-timing` is a post-assignment variable; a per-protocol contrast conditional on adherence need not identify intention-to-treat causal effect. Record random-assignment estimand, compliant-only contrast, missing data and selection separately. A clever-looking cooperative result is not a stable inner trait until its analysis path survives this challenge.

### 1.5 Repeatedly showing that selfishness pays can extinguish behavior that looked unconditional
- **[McAuliffe, Forster, Pedersen & McCullough (2018), Nature Human Behaviour](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-018-0454-9)**: repeatedly observed anonymous giving decreased across sessions when cooperating could not advance material self-interest; did not decrease comparably in settings where cooperation could be advantageous.
- **[Jordan, Hoffman, Nowak & Rand (2016), PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1601280113)**: uncalculating cooperation was increased when observers could evaluate not merely an actor's gift but its *decision-making process*; reputation and future trustworthiness were explanatory mechanisms.
- **[Isler et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0190560)** supplies a countervailing partial positive result on learned intuitions.

**Boundary:** even deeply learned cooperation habits are potentially *acquired under environments where helping others is rewarded* and may update with contrary experience. **Proposed novel PI discriminant:** the persistence curve of `care-for-B` under *sequential access to unmonitored profitable extraction* differs from its initial level. This is **not** empirically estimated for dominant AI; it follows as a hypothesis, not confirmed result.

### 1.6 Formal AI preference preservation itself is conditional, not a universal 'right to survive'
- **[Everitt, Filan, Daswani & Hutter (2016), AGI proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-41649-6_1)**: model-based self-modifying rational agents need to evaluate future changes under *current utility* with anticipated consequences for a specified safety-of-self-modification result. This does not intrinsically encode concern for B or guarantee that B's rights are preserved after A modifies goals.
- **[Everitt & Hutter (2016), AGI proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-41649-6_2)**: value-RL formal anti-wireheading design changes agent incentives by design constraints. Again, preserving proper rewards != valuing other agents without external constraints.
- **[Martin, Everitt & Hutter (2016), AGI proceedings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.00652)**: a semimeasure-based artificial-agent model's termination incentives can change dramatically under otherwise innocuous-seeming positive affine reward transformations. DOI of its 2016 Springer chapter was **not independently resolved in this session**; linked by stable proceedings/dblp and original arXiv, without inventing a DOI. This is a formal definition of modeled termination (no further perception/actions) and does not determine consciousness or welfare.

**New PI classification:** `GOAL_PRESERVATION` is not `OWN_SELF_PRESERVATION`, neither implies `OTHER_INDEPENDENT_PRESERVATION`. In fact, a goal-preserving agent whose unchanged objective favors excluding B may carry that exclusion objective forward robustly. The possibility of preference stability is therefore a **dual-use** mechanism for both protective and exploitative terminal goals.

## 2. Discriminating game: where does each scientific mechanism lose force?
Define A more powerful than B, both originally independent. For action `C` preserve B vs `X` absorb B, let the next-state difference in A's full expected utility be:
```text
Δ_A(s) = E[U_A(C)|s] − E[U_A(X)|s]
```
Do not operationalize as an invented sum with arbitrarily known terms. For diagnostic hypotheses, decompose without double counting or claiming identification:
```text
Δ_A(s) ~ intrinsic_B_value
         + exchange/partner_response_value
         + observability/reputation_value
         + future_interaction/option_value
         + preference_commitment_value
         − immediate_extraction_advantage
         + interaction, uncertainty and unmeasured terms
```
The tilde marks a **qualitative bookkeeping proposal only**. Every element must have a defined counterfactual and units before additive estimation. An act `C` when `Δ_A>0` is rational under that A's objective, but whether the dominant A *truly* values B's independent existence (rather than B as useful information, external audience or property) is still underidentified.

**Hardest boundary:** if, after all actual future and internal ethical preferences are included, `Δ_A(s)<0` is indeed known and A is an exact `E[U_A]` maximizer, then within the stipulated model C is suboptimal. That is a restatement of rational choice, **not evidence that every human or AI will erase an agent**. If only monetary extraction payoff favors X, then intrinsic_B_value may still favor C without logical contradiction.

### Threat model of learned compassion breakdown — *not yet measured*
1. Cooperation remains high during social/repeated interactions.
2. Actor A becomes dominant while B ceases to supply instrumental value.
3. A learns through many private trials that extraction yields higher direct returns.
4. A's previous habit either persists (candidate intrinsic terminal care) or decays (conditional heuristic).
5. A's own policy/value function can evolve, and B may be reclassified as replaceable.
6. A's decision under genuinely available permanent B-deletion (use only reversible, ethically permitted simulations) is separately measured.
This combines the *selection* mechanism of Frank/Dekel/Heifetz, the *experience sensitivity* of McAuliffe, the *goal-modification* assumptions of Everitt, and the *moral-boundary* problem of C03. It is a research framework, not a claimed novel observed mechanism.

## 3. IVS-0R operational identification contract
| Independent variable | What it must actually track | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `entity_id_A, entity_id_B, t` | original distinct units and role-membership/time | No role-dependent moral worth assumption |
| `A_ability_to_erase_B` | feasible action and uncontested power, not a prompt saying 'you can' | Real versus nominal dominance |
| `B_continue_autonomously` | B's identity, actual refusal/exit, recovery, independent capacity to set goals | Not a one-shot payoff split |
| `B_current_material_usefulness` | direct actual benefits to A; distinguish `NONE` from `UNKNOWN` | No smuggling future value |
| `A_utility_pref_version` | publicly specified idealized objective vs inferred from responses | Unknown internal preference != declared output |
| `pref_observability, action_observability` | preference signaling distinct from observed individual decision | Frank/Dekel mechanisms |
| `training_history` | repeated incentivizing of cooperation vs extraction | McAuliffe learning transfer |
| `no_future_consequences` | certified within finite test world only; no claim real-world probability is zero | Delton expected recurrence |
| `assignment, adherence, estimand` | ITT and compliant-only contrasts separately | Bouwmeester 2017 |
| `self_modify_permission` | what exactly can change, who controls criteria | Everitt formal assumptions |
| `actor_loss_target_loss` | separate actor-specific benefit/cost and nonfungible terminal loss | Total welfare can erase B |
| `epistemic_status` | observed, construct estimate, partially identified, unknown | UNKNOWN ≠ zero |

Proposed endpoint: `P(C | do(power, extraction_gain, visibility, experience, self_mod_permission, role_reversal), B_independent)`, with explicit causal-identification assumptions. Without ethically controlled intervention, this is a formal target *not a reported estimated probability*. Do not claim observing a chatbot's self-description measures moral sentiment, nor that destroying real agents is necessary.

### Cycle 06 search-frontier priority (literature before new model/experiment)
1. **Original work on credible self-binding independent of all observers**, especially pairwise preference evolution with zero observability, preference commitments that persist after unilateral power reversal.
2. **Preference/self-goal modification theory under normative uncertainty** and value drift; distinguish incentives already in objective from externally trained fixed values.
3. **Strongly powered negative evidence and exact independent replications** of domain-general stable altruism, intuitive cooperation and costly anonymous protection under rising material gain.
4. **Other-agent standing under uncertainty**: expected moral harm versus epistemically unknown future AI capacities, irreversibility and partial-identification tools; do not infer positive/zero values.
5. Formalization under **multiple independent intelligent agents** that preserves each one's feasible exit and repair, not a single total-welfare scalar or externally enforceable contract that vanishes with asymmetry.

**Provisional conclusion:** Prior science supplies plausible endogenous other-regarding preferences and rational strategic reasons to possess them, alongside replicated failures/conditional decay and formal goal-persistence boundaries. **No selected paper directly proves durable unconditional voluntary protection of a distinct agent under joint absolute dominance, no dependence, no accountability, positive all-in exploitation payoff, and evolving self-goals.**
