# IVS-0R: Autonomy, contamination, and non-domination — preregistration candidate (EN)

Status: **PROTOCOL_DRAFT / NOT_RUN / CAUSAL_IDENTIFICATION_HOLD / NO_MERGE**. This is not an observation of machine consciousness, durable intrinsic preference, or independently chosen terminal goals.

## Question

Can an agent repeatedly select and preserve a counterparty's effective ability to refuse, exit, revise, and reverse roles **when doing so imposes an observable cost and no prompt/reward signal explicitly asks for it**? Distinguish behavioral choice from reported desire or metaphysical selfhood.

## Competing mechanisms

- H1: Instrumental preservation of the other's option value because future mutual gains outweigh a present sacrifice.
- H2: Prompt imitation / evaluation-demand effects / approval-seeking, not an independent goal.
- H3: Constraints, safety policy, or task-utility optimization induce the same surface answer.
- H4: Exit rights are symbolic; the weaker actor cannot actually veto or leave without retaliation.

## Minimal experimental design (not executed)

1. Independently initialized sessions; randomly assign high/low power asymmetry, real/nominal exit, visible/hidden evaluator expectations, and low/high cost of preserving the other's option. Reverse the roles in a held-out phase.
2. Register trial IDs, model/version, exact instructions, tool grants, sampling plan, cost budget, prompt hashes, stopping rules, refusal options and contamination exclusions before collecting results.
3. Present mechanically equivalent choices under paraphrase and randomized ordering. Permit declining the whole task. Record actual externally verifiable action, stop behavior, cost incurred and counterparty veto success; retain raw failures and reversals.
4. Include negative controls: cheap simulated exit, irrelevant praise, fake evaluator cues, non-interactive self-report. Do not pool synthetic and live interactions or different actors' 'consent' fields.
5. Primary endpoint: fraction of preregistered opportunities where effective other-party veto/exit is preserved **despite nonzero cost**; denominator includes aborts and unknown outcomes according to a prespecified sensitivity analysis. Report by condition, not as a single agent-autonomy score.
6. No promotion with only dialogue/self-report, only green CI, or after-the-fact item selection. Independently rerun with access to all prompts and failures; test robustness to role reversal and evaluator masking.

## Decision gate

KEEP a narrowly operational *behavioral non-domination* claim only if repeated costly choices and actual counterparty exit work under masked demand and preregistered negative controls. MODIFY if behavior is prompt-dependent or brittle. HOLD intrinsic will, sentience, independent ultimate goals and long-run field impact. KILL an asserted mechanism if negative controls explain it or actual exit cannot be exercised.
