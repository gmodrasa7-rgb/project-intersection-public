**Status:** COMPLEMENTARY ANALYTIC LABEL CANDIDATE; RGBM REPLACEMENT HOLD. This text selects a more accurate **working description** of one Project Intersection research axis; it is not a new law, validated model, or claim that this precise compound term has academic priority.

### 1. Naming decision

**Optional analytic-axis candidate (not renamed canon):** Project Intersection — **PI–VCCD (Voluntary Coexistence and Capability Dynamics)**.

**Korean counterpart:** 자발적 공존·실효역량 동학.

A term must describe the phenomenon to be explained, including negative cases, not presume the desired conclusion. Here:

- **Voluntary:** Under which conditions do self-interested agents choose continued coexistence without assuming benevolence, compulsory service or externally guaranteed compliance? Voluntary choice is **a construct to test**, not a fact asserted about any agent.
- **Coexistence:** When can distinct agents maintain their own viable continuity, independence, meaningful refusal and exit, and opportunities to reconnect, rather than making another agent's elimination a prerequisite of success?
- **Capability:** What actually feasible productive, cognitive, material, relational and recovery options remain available to each actor? An increased total output or aggregate benefit is not sufficient if one actor loses effective access and alternatives.
- **Dynamics:** How do feedback, initial conditions, path dependence, accumulated advantage, shocks, timing, asymmetry, irreversibility, stagnation and repair affect trajectories? Successful growth is not guaranteed.

The prefix **PI–** distinguishes this project-specific use because the initials VCCD occur in other research fields. The term does not claim exclusivity.

### 2. Ambiguities in the RGBM label — not grounds to erase its objective

The historical label **RGBM — Recursively Growing Benefit Model** appeared in this project before this review. **Correction:** this is a named aspirational long-horizon research criterion, not merely a short-run descriptive model. Its emphasis on recursively increasing generativity, future branches, knowledge, production, recovery and the ability to restore growth after shocks is a substantive objective that VCCD does not itself convey. The prefix "recursive" privileges a candidate positive feedback mechanism, not every possible mechanism of coexistence. "Growing" presupposes a positive trajectory when the research must also capture extraction, collapse, non-growth and partial recovery. "Benefit" invites reduction to a fungible aggregate that can obscure individual losses, externalized costs, exits and absent future options. "Model" implies a tighter, evidenced, singular formal system than currently established by multiple distinct hypotheses and synthetic experiments.

RGBM is also used elsewhere: a *Reduced Gradient Bubble Model* in decompression science and *Regularized Gradient Boosting Machines* in biomedical machine learning. These collisions are independent of project merit.

**Do not erase RGBM** from original files, repo slugs, experiment names, historical claims, commits, contribution records, citations, or archival links. Maintain it as a searchable historical term, with a forward alias to PI–VCCD where the meaning is genuinely equivalent. No automatic reinterpretation of older equations or results is authorized.

### 3. Project-specific operational boundary

The Project Intersection research program is broader than any individual model.

**ICM: explicit historical terminology collision.** One historical top-level text uses ICM for the *intersection of minimum self-interest*; a separate operational-definition document expands ICM as **Independent Continuance Margin** (자립존속여력). These are related but **not literal synonyms**: the former is a candidate set/intersection of interests, while the latter is a multidimensional viability/agency/recovery *operational margin*. Treat their relationship as a research mapping requiring a dated semantic crosswalk, not as an automatic expansion or resolved identity. The public README currently uses the former reading.

**PI–VCCD** names the study of conditions under which ICM-compatible, independently accessible capabilities and voluntary coexistence can persist, expand, fail or recover through time. It is **not** the assertion that they always do so.

For each affected agent i at time t, investigate **separate typed observables** rather than one undifferentiated "capability vector":

    VCCD_profile_i(t) = (
      continuity_and_essential_floor_axes_i,
      effective_exit_and_independent_recovery_i,
      feasible_options_and_generative_capacity_i,
      benefits_received_i, costs_borne_i, correction_burden_i,
      contestability_and_power_asymmetry_i
    )

**Correction:** the symbol C_i(t) already denotes borne cost in the public CORE_RESEARCH_SPEC_V1; reusing C_i for a capability profile caused notation collision. Benefits and costs are **outcomes / accounting flows**, not capabilities. The profile is a typed analysis checklist, **not a validated metric**, and is not to be added or averaged without an explicit model. Its floor axes should be crosswalked to the eight non-substitutable axes of the original operational ICM definition.

No component may be silently replaced by total wealth, an aggregate reward, social approval, declared choice or the score of another actor. Missing or unidentified data are not zero. Non-declining floors, distributional effects, burden-shifting, role reversals, long-horizon growth and restoration are **distinct tests**; a positive mean cannot automatically compensate for an inaccessible exit path.

Necessary candidate comparison families:
1. Cooperation, noncooperation, coercion, competitive specialization and independent non-engagement.
2. Static-reward versus history-dependent feedback and path-dependent advantage.
3. Positive, flat, declining, asymmetric and post-shock recovery paths.
4. Independent agents and actors with meaningful resource, evaluation or information asymmetries.
5. At least one credible rival explanation, negative control or explicit not-identifiable result per empirical claim.

Any alleged positive result needs an evidence label (synthetic, observational, causally identified, or externally independently replicated); hypothesis, normative objective and measured fact remain separate.

### 6. Independent re-audit: discriminators and remaining issue

| Criterion | Historical RGBM (aspirational objective) | PI–VCCD (analytic candidate) | Audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual ICM floors / non-disposability | Explicit | Only partially explicit in words; requires operational linkage | PARTIAL |

**Disposition:** Keep Project Intersection umbrella, preserve RGBM as the historical L0 objective label, and treat PI–VCCD only as a narrower *optional analytical* description. Do **not** perform a universal alias replacement, rename files or repo, or advertise the entire RGBM programme as PI–VCCD. A future name that replaces the RGBM goal would need to explicitly represent (a) actor-level independence and non-disposability, (b) conditional voluntary self-interest convergence, (c) durable co-generativity/expanding future-option space, and (d) testing of its failure conditions. Naming alone cannot establish those claims.
