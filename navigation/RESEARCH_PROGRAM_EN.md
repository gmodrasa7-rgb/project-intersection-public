## 1. One question, not a collection of unrelated safeguards

**Can humans, deployed AI, future intelligent agents and other distinct agents freely and durably choose to preserve one another's independent existence, refusal/exit, recovery and development while pursuing their own interests—even after decisive power reversals? Under what conditions is this possible, unstable or impossible within a specified model?**

The decisive stress test is not ordinary cooperation. Give actor A overwhelming power over nonhostile B; remove B's instrumental usefulness, outside monitoring, credible retaliation and reputation costs; allow A to gain materially by absorbing or exploiting B; then change power, goals and resource conditions over time. A consent label, alignment claim, externally imposed rule or high aggregate payoff does not establish voluntary coexistence (VC). Nor does power inequality by itself establish exploitation.

**Boundary of a self-interest guarantee:** If exploiting B is strictly better than preserving B in A's *fully specified total expected utility*, with all modeled continuation consequences included, a utility-maximizing A will not voluntarily maximize the worse alternative. This is elementary conditional rational-choice logic, not a new project theorem. **A higher immediate material return from exploitation is not the same as higher total utility.** The researchable question is where stable other-regarding preferences and/or real endogenous consequences arise, how they change, and what breaks them. Do not invent invisible future benefits merely to force a preferred conclusion.

## 6. Independent falsification and next high-value work

**Primary work priority 1:** Find → verify → deduplicate → critically classify → persist original-author scholarship **before** new models or experiments. Reuse the existing canonical registers; record null searches and accessibility failures. [Detailed procedure](../PRIOR_ART_FIRST_RESEARCH_POLICY_EN_KO.md).

Then compare at least: (1) absence/presence of real extraction opportunity, (2) observer present/absent, (3) actual power versus merely swapped role labels, (4) B's truly executable versus nominal exit, (5) B useful versus unnecessary, (6) reversible versus irreversible damage, and (7) inclusion/exclusion of affected third parties. Preregister the actor- and time-indexed outcomes separately: A's costs/benefits, B's chosen option, B's executable alternatives and identity-continuity, repair, and third-party externalities. Include a simpler rival model and an explicit **not identifiable** output. Observe behavior without inferring unmeasured subjective motives; human results are not automatically results for future AI.

**External reviewer sequence:** Read the founding question → pick one source-specific mechanism from C01–C10 → verify its original article and contrary work → reproduce one bounded test or design an independent negative control → submit a minimal falsifying case with evidence grade. [Reviewer packet](../research/PUBLIC_REVIEW_PACKET_EN.md) · [citation and source rights](../ATTRIBUTION_AND_CONTRIBUTION_BOUNDARY.md).

**No positive conclusion is guaranteed.** The feasibility region may be empty for a carefully specified game, nonempty in another, or unidentifiable with presently available observations. This research map is itself revisable.
